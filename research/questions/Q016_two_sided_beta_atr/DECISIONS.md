## Standing rules proposed
- The reference point for a **symmetric, direction-free band endpoint** (±k ATR from a single anchor) is the pick-night regular-session close for picks and controls alike, because an open basis hands the overnight gap to one side of a two-sided conjunction. This is DP-11 applied to a case DP-11 does not literally name; if Haci confirms it, it generalises to any future ±k ATR question.
- When a hypothesis names **two conditioning variables** for the same metric, both are registered as co-primaries under one BH correction rather than one being chosen — choosing would require the in-sample direction the sealed window exists to exclude.
- **Added at record.** When a question's **maturity window dominates its accrual rate** — every exposure-driven floor date lands before the registered window end plus maturity, as here (2026-10-05 / 2027-01-04 / 2027-02-01 against 2027-02-22) — the schedule rule is **`fixed`**, the decision date is the window end plus maturity plus the one-week margin, and a measured run-rate *faster* than the projection changes nothing. DP-43 already forbids pulling a date in; this states the positive form, which is what a 60-session question needs, and it stops a fast rate being read as a licence to shorten the window.
- **Added at record.** A **control-pool symbol whose price history stops mid-freeze** while it keeps appearing in `sas_candidates` (the CTRA signature) is dropped and counted as a measurement failure, never imputed or back-filled, and the successor freeze carries a **targeted scan for that signature** as a completion criterion rather than waiting for an exposure count to surface it. This generalises to every question with a matched-control arm (Q006, Q009, Q011, Q015, Q016).
