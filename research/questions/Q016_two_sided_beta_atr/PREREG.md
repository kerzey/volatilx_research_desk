## 1. Hypothesis (plain English, one sentence)

**The most volatile picks round-trip more often: among the picks published on a given night, those with the highest 60-day beta — and, separately, those with the highest ATR% — go on to touch *both* +2 ATR and −2 ATR from the pick-night close within 60 trading sessions more often than the least volatile picks published the same night, and more often than same-night unpublished candidates with the same volatility profile.**

This is Haci's own belief, filed as H-059: *"high-beta / high-ATR picks are two-sided more often, touching both +2 ATR and −2 ATR within 60 sessions — baseline: low-beta tercile of picks, and beta-matched same-night unpublished candidates."* It matters to how he trades: a name that reaches its target and then hands it back is a different trade from a name that grinds one way, and the two want different structures and different partial-exit discipline. Nothing on the platform predicts, prints or acts on round-trip risk today.

**Why it is registered even though the in-sample read went the other way.** EXPLORE_001 §D measured the same metric (`±2 ATR / 60`) on the April–May in-sample nights and found the belief **not supported in ATR units**: the high-beta pick tercile realized the widest range (6.1 ATR) but its two-sided rate was 0.42 against 0.32 for low-beta and 0.44 for mid-beta — no monotone gradient — and the *low*-ATR tercile was the most two-sided (0.54 vs 0.39 for high-ATR). April–May was a one-way tape, in which extra range shows up on one side only. June–August was not. H-059 exists precisely because the tape changed, and the honest test is a sealed, forward window with the metric fixed in advance. **The in-sample direction is not used to choose the primary endpoint, the sign, or which of the two volatility variables is tested** — both are registered as co-primaries (§4, §7), and every test is two-sided: a result beyond MPE in the in-sample direction is a confirmed finding with that sign, and it kills the belief rather than confirming it.

**What is *not* claimed here.** Two-sidedness is a statement about the *path*, not about money. No stop is assumed anywhere (rule 5, DP-02); a round trip costs a holder nothing unless he was stopped or exited into it. §9 binds what any confirmed verdict is allowed to change.

## 3. Baseline(s) — what this must beat

There is always a baseline, and here there are three, because "high-beta picks round-trip often" is meaningless without "and other things did not, on the same nights".

- **B1 (primary, and H-059's own first baseline): the low tercile of the same night's published picks.** Same tape, same night, same publication process, same 60-session clock, same symmetric ±2 ATR band scaled to each pick's own ATR. This is "Y's rate when X did not happen, in the same regime" in its cleanest available form, and it is the paired contrast the two primaries estimate (§4). Band **M** is printed beside it so a non-monotone pattern (which is what the in-sample read showed: 0.42 / 0.44 / 0.32 for H / M / L) is visible rather than hidden by an H-minus-L difference.
- **B2 (H-059's second baseline, and the rule-5 distance-matched control): same-night unpublished candidates matched on the volatility profile.** For each eligible pick, the **10 nearest same-night non-published `sas_candidates` rows** (the complement of the DP-28 predicate, dark-lane rows included) on `beta60` / `atr_pct` / `runup20` from `prices_daily_split` bars dated ≤ t, standardized by the night's cross-sectional median and MAD, Euclidean distance, with replacement across picks, ties by symbol ascending — the Q006 §3 construction, reused verbatim so the two questions' control cohorts are comparable. **Distance-matching is automatic here and is the reason this endpoint is unusually clean:** the level is defined as ±2 × the control's *own* ATR14 from the control's *own* close, so every arm carries a target at the identical ATR distance by construction, with no synthetic-target arithmetic and no dependence on where an LLM printed a ladder. B2 answers the question B1 cannot: *is a volatility gradient in round-trip rate a property of **picks**, or of **stocks** with those volatility characteristics?* It is descriptive except for one clause in §8 (the sign-agreement gate), for the exposure reason in §10.8.
  **Measured coverage (`STEWARD_Q016_exposure.md` §5, matured slice):** the B2 pool is **458 rows** (201 distinct symbols) over the 11 matured nights; **458/458** are carried in the price freeze with ≥ 60 prior daily bars, **455/458** also have a `C_t` and a complete 60-bar forward window, and **88/88** eligible picks can be given a full 10-nearest-neighbour match. B2 is therefore computable today and §8 clause 7 is evaluable on the matured slice. **The one named gap is `CTRA`, whose daily bars in `p001_daily_split` stop at 2026-05-06 although it keeps appearing as an unpublished candidate through 2026-06-11**, costing the 3 rows above (2026-06-04, 06-05, 06-11) their `C_t`, forward bars and `runup20`. Such rows are **dropped from the control pool and counted in the funnel — never back-filled or imputed**, the same measurement-failure treatment §2 applies to treatment rows. This is a genuine mid-freeze coverage gap for one symbol, not a maturity or boundary effect; it changes no verdict here and is disclosed as a threat in §10.8. Clause 7 remains conditional on the successor freezes (§5, R2) carrying the same coverage for every future in-window night, which cannot be confirmed before those symbols exist.
- **B3 (the unconditional rate, descriptive):** the two-sided rate of all eligible published picks pooled, and of the full same-night unpublished candidate universe pooled. This is the "what does a stock do in 60 sessions" number that every reported rate must be read against, and it is what stops a 40%-ish two-sided rate from sounding like a finding on its own.

## 4. Objective metric (rule 5 — price path, measured the way it is traded)

**Reference point (DECISIONS.md #1, DECIDED).** `X = C_t`, the pick-night regular-session close from `prices_daily_split`, for picks and matched controls alike; both levels are fixed at that close and never moved. One basis for picks and for matched controls, because only the close exists on the control side and rule 5 requires the same basis on both sides (DP-11's construction, DP-03(a)'s after-hours fill proxy). It is also the basis on which ATR14 is measured and the basis EXPLORE_001 used for the in-sample read of this exact metric, so the sealed result is comparable to the read that produced the hypothesis. The **next-session-open basis (DP-03(b))** is computed and printed for both primaries as a named sensitivity.

**The two levels, per pick, fixed at the close and never moved:**
`U = X + 2 × ATR14_t` and `D = X − 2 × ATR14_t` (DP-25 — 2 ATR is H-059's own unit and cut, taken as written; the registrar does not re-unit a hypothesis).

**Grading window:** sessions **t+1 .. t+60**, regular-session daily bars only (DP-25 — 60 sessions is H-059's own clock. DP-09's 20-session spread clock does not apply: the primary level here is not L3 and the question is not about a spread. A shorter window was **not** chosen to reach a decision date sooner — DP-43, DP-45).

**The three events, per pick:**
- **TOUCH_U = 1** iff some session `s ∈ [t+1, t+60]` has `high_s ≥ U`.
- **TOUCH_D = 1** iff some session `s ∈ [t+1, t+60]` has `low_s ≤ D`.
- **TWO_SIDED = TOUCH_U ∧ TOUCH_D**, the primary event.

**Ordering rules, stated once and applied by `eval.py` with no judgment:**
1. **A gap through a level is a touch** at that level. There is no "already passed at entry" case: both levels lie 2 ATR away from `X` in opposite directions at the moment of entry, so neither can be through at entry, and the window starts at t+1 (DP-26's gap-through clause is satisfied vacuously — a t+1 gap is a genuine move after entry, not a pre-entry pass).
2. **Both levels stay live for all 60 sessions.** Touching one does not retire the other, and neither is an exit (rule 5, DP-02). There is no stop in this question.
3. **Order of the two touches is descriptive only.** Where both are first touched on the same session, `prices_hourly_raw` orders them for published symbols; where one hourly bar holds both, or the symbol has no hourly bars (the whole B2 pool, §10.9), the pair counts **adverse-first** — **DP-27**, the reading less favourable to a holder. The count resolved by this rule and the opposite reading are both printed. **None of this touches the primary endpoint**, which is a conjunction and is invariant to order — a deliberate design choice that makes the primary computable from daily bars alone.

**Primary endpoints (two co-primaries, pre-specified, BH-corrected within the question and across the family — §7; DECISIONS.md #2, DECIDED: both `beta60` and `atr_pct` are registered as co-primaries under DP-25 and DP-42, and **neither may be demoted at the decision pass**).** For night `t`, `mean_H[·]` is the mean over that night's band-H eligible picks and `mean_L[·]` over band-L:

- **P1 — the beta gradient.** `Δ1_t = mean_H,beta[TWO_SIDED] − mean_L,beta[TWO_SIDED]`; estimand `m1 = mean_t Δ1_t` over P1 contributing nights, in **percentage points**.
- **P2 — the ATR% gradient.** `Δ2_t = mean_H,atr[TWO_SIDED] − mean_L,atr[TWO_SIDED]`; estimand `m2 = mean_t Δ2_t` over P2 contributing nights, in **percentage points**.

Both are **two-sided**. H-059 predicts `m1 > 0` and `m2 > 0`; EXPLORE_001's in-sample read points to `m2 < 0` and to no monotone `m1`. A result beyond MPE with either sign is a confirmed finding with that sign (§8, §9).

**Mandatory decomposition, printed with every primary (descriptive, never decides).** A conjunction can rise because a tercile moves more in *both* directions, or merely because it moves more in *one*; the in-sample read is that picks realize extra range one-directionally, and this is the read that distinguishes the two:
`TWO_SIDED rate`, `TOUCH_U rate`, `TOUCH_D rate`, `P(TOUCH_D | TOUCH_U)`, realized 60-session **range in ATR** (`(max high − min low) / ATR14_t`), and the **forward/backward realized-volatility ratio** (60-session realized vol after t over 60-session realized vol to t) — each per tercile, per variable, with its own CI. The conditional factor is labelled *"conditions on a post-entry path event; descriptive, does not decide"*.

**Secondary, descriptive, never decides:**
- **B2 and the control-adjusted gradients:** picks minus their matched controls, per tercile, and `(H excess) − (L excess)`. The control-adjusted gradients carry one §8 clause and nothing more.
- **The ladder-anchored companion**, so that any §9 line can be written on levels the platform actually prints: first touch of **L3** (`lane_plans.swing_trading.targets[0]`) and of the **printed swing stop** within t+1..t+60, and the share of picks touching both, per tercile — on the sub-population carrying a swing lane, with that sub-population's own counts printed.
- **The threshold and clock grid**, registered here so it can never be searched at the decision pass: `±1.5 ATR / 20 sessions`, `±2 ATR / 20`, `±2 ATR / 40`, `±3 ATR / 60`. **These are sensitivities, not endpoints. Only `±2 ATR / 60` decides**, whatever the grid shows; a report sentence that leans on a grid cell is out of bounds.
- The **pooled expanding-window tercile** version of both primaries (absolute cuts rather than within-night relative ones) — the read on §10.4.
- The **next-open basis** version of both primaries.
- The **bivariate cell** rates: beta tercile × ATR% tercile (9 cells), reported only at ≥ 20 contributing nights — the read on §10.5.
- Sessions from entry to the first touch of each side; MFE and MAE in ATR from `X`; which side came first (DP-27 as above); direction-adjusted favourable-first and adverse-first shares.
- Sub-cells: bull / bear, `best_timeframe` lane, score band (80–85 / 85–90 / 90+), tape stratum, regime label — reported only at ≥ 20 contributing nights, SUPPRESSED below.
- Close-to-close return from `X` at T+20 and T+60 — **fixed-horizon return is secondary and descriptive by rule 5 and never decides.**

**Quotability.** The primary basis is a 60-session window (**W60**), which is the quotable basis under rule 12 — but nothing here is quotable before PROSPECTIVELY_CONFIRMED (rule 10, §8). Every grid cell on a 20- or 40-session clock is labelled **NON_QUOTABLE**.

**Inference.** Night-level (rule 6); control rows never add to inferential n.
- **p-value (decides significance):** a **within-night permutation of the tercile labels** across the night's eligible picks, preserving each night's band sizes, 10,000 permutations, seed 20260913. This is exact under the null "the tercile label is exchangeable within the night" and is **immune to the cross-night dependence that heavily overlapping 60-session windows create** (§10.3) — the reason it, and not a bootstrap p, is primary here. A paired sign-flip permutation on the night contrasts is printed alongside.
- **CI (decides the excludes-zero clause):** stationary block bootstrap over the ordered contributing nights, **expected block length 30 sessions** (half the forward window — the same convention Q011/Q015 apply at 10 sessions for a 20-session window; fixed here and not chosen after seeing results), 2,000 resamples. The date-clustered bootstrap CI is printed alongside and is expected to be narrower; **the block-bootstrap interval is the one that decides.**
- Every estimate prints: n(contributing nights, per endpoint), n(contributing nights dated after the lock commit), n(eligible picks per band), n(TWO_SIDED), n(TOUCH_U), n(TOUCH_D), n(control rows and control-set sizes), n(ungradeable), n(immature), n(excluded, by reason), and the post-match standardized mean differences for B2.

## 8. Decision rule (numeric, written before unsealing)

`m1` and `m2` are in **percentage points of TWO_SIDED rate** (high tercile minus low tercile, paired within night), over each endpoint's own contributing nights.

**MPE: +5.0 pp for both endpoints** (DP-20, DP-44 — the standing number for a control-adjusted or within-night touch-rate endpoint). No uplift is proposed: this is a within-night paired contrast on one tape, the Q011/Q015 structure. A gradient smaller than 5 pp would not change how a position is structured.

Both endpoints are two-sided; the verdict carries its sign.

- **HISTORICALLY_CONFIRMED** requires **all** of:
  1. **≥ 80 contributing nights** on that endpoint's own definition **and ≥ 30 contributing nights dated after the lock commit** (DP-21, DP-24), **and ≥ 20 contributing nights for any sub-cell that is reported** — a sub-cell below floor is **SUPPRESSED** (counts only) and does **not by itself** make the endpoint INCONCLUSIVE;
  2. `|m| > 5.0 pp`;
  3. the **block-bootstrap** 95% CI (§4) excludes 0;
  4. BH **q ≤ 0.10** within the question (m = 2) **and** within F7, computed on the within-night permutation p;
  5. the point estimate has the **same sign in both halves**, and neither half is beyond MPE in the opposite sign;
  6. no tape stratum with ≥ 20 contributing nights is beyond MPE in the opposite sign;
  7. **the control-adjusted gradient (B2, §3) is not beyond MPE in the opposite sign** — the "is this stocks or is this picks" gate (DECISIONS.md #3, DECIDED: the hard-clause reading, DP-45). **If B2 cannot be computed** for a night because the price freeze does not carry the unpublished symbols' 60 prior and 60 forward bars (§10.8), this clause is **not evaluable and the endpoint is INCONCLUSIVE**, never CONFIRMED on B1 alone. Individual control rows lost to a truncated history (the CTRA signature, §3) are dropped and counted, not imputed, and do not by themselves make the clause unevaluable; the clause fails only where a night's control cohort cannot be built at all.
- **NULL:** floors met **and** the 95% CI includes 0 **and** `|m| < 5.0 pp`. "How volatile a pick is at selection does not tell you whether it will round-trip" is a real finding about a belief Haci trades on, and it is ledgered with the same care as a confirmation.
- **INCONCLUSIVE:** anything else — halves disagreeing in sign; a tape stratum or the control-adjusted gradient beyond MPE in the opposite sign; B2 not evaluable; `0 < |m| ≤ 5.0 pp` with the CI excluding 0 ("real but below MPE", rule 6); or the CI including 0 with `|m| ≥ 5.0 pp`. **A SUPPRESSED sub-cell is not by itself INCONCLUSIVE**; what bites is a *reported* sub-cell below floor, which must not happen. **A gate shortfall is not INCONCLUSIVE either**: it fires the single DP-13 extension, then DEFERRED (§5).
- **What a confirmed endpoint does and does not mean (binding on the report).** A CONFIRMED-positive P1 means **"a larger share of the night's highest-beta picks touched both +2 ATR and −2 ATR from the pick-night close inside 60 sessions than the night's lowest-beta picks did"**. It does **not** mean those picks are worse, or that they gave back what they got: it is a statement about a symmetric band, with no stop and no exit anywhere in it (DP-02). Any sentence of the "high-beta picks hand it back" kind must quote `P(TOUCH_D | TOUCH_U)` and the `TOUCH_U` / `TOUCH_D` split beside it and label them descriptive. A confirmed **negative** endpoint means the opposite gradient, not "no effect", and is reported with its sign.
- **PROSPECTIVELY_CONFIRMED:** reachable from this run by design (§5) — it requires **≥ 30 contributing nights dated after this file's lock commit** (DP-24, DP-21), frozen in the successor manifests and never inspected earlier, reproducing the sign of the confirming endpoint under the unmodified `eval.py`. The clause does not weaken: if the window cannot supply 30, the DP-13 extension fires and then DEFERRED — the 30 is never reduced. No subscriber-facing statement before that (rule 10).

## 10. Known threats to validity (registrar's own list)

1. **TWO_SIDED is a conjunction, and the interesting failure mode is one-directional range.** A tercile can score higher simply by moving more in *one* direction while the other side is reached by noise, and the in-sample read is exactly that (widest range, no two-sidedness gradient). The §4 decomposition — `TOUCH_U`, `TOUCH_D`, the conditional, the range in ATR — is mandatory, and §8 binds what a confirmed endpoint may be said to mean.
2. **The ATR denominator is the point and also a hazard.** ±2 ATR is a *wider absolute* band for a high-ATR name, so the endpoint deliberately asks whether volatile names round-trip **relative to their own volatility**. In percent terms volatile names obviously swing more; the percent version is not registered as an endpoint and the report may not drift into it. Separately, ATR14 at t may systematically under-predict forward volatility for high-beta names in a vol-expanding tape, which would inflate the high tercile mechanically — the forward/backward realized-vol ratio is printed per tercile for exactly this read.
3. **Overlapping 60-session windows are the worst dependence structure the desk has registered.** Adjacent nights' outcome windows share ~98% of their sessions, so night counts overstate independent information badly. Two pre-specified protections: the **within-night permutation** p-value, which is exact under label exchangeability and does not assume independence across nights; and a **block bootstrap with 30-session blocks** for the CI, fixed here and not chosen after seeing results. Even so the CI will rest on few effective blocks and may be unstable — this raises the risk of INCONCLUSIVE, which is the acceptable direction of error, and it is the reason a within-night paired contrast (which removes the common market factor) is the primary rather than a pooled rate.
4. **Terciles are within-night and therefore relative.** On a night whose whole slate is high-beta, band L is still high-beta in absolute terms, which compresses the contrast toward NULL. The pooled expanding-window version is printed as a sensitivity; it is not the primary because absolute cuts would let a tape shift move whole nights between bands and would reintroduce the confound the pairing removes.
5. **Beta and ATR% are correlated, so P1 and P2 are not independent tests.** BH across m = 2 does not repair this. The 9-cell bivariate table is printed where cells clear 20 nights, and §8 requires each endpoint to clear every clause on its own; §9 refuses to ship anything when the two disagree in sign.
6. **Regime confound, and the label is assigned before the window that matters.** A 60-session outcome window from a June 2026 night ends in September; from a November night it ends in February 2027. The stratum label is legal only at 16:05 ET on the pick night (rule 14), so it describes the tape *entering* the window, not the tape *inside* it. The halves and the tape strata are the only protection, and the whole window may turn out to be one tape — in which case rule 7's "a result that only holds in one period is not a result" bites, and the honest verdict is INCONCLUSIVE.
7. **Right-censoring and maturity.** Immature nights are excluded from the trading calendar, never graded as non-touch, and the ungradeable rule in §2 is asymmetric on purpose: a pick whose two-sidedness is already established by observed bars is kept, one whose missing bars could have contained either touch is dropped and counted.
8. **B2 depends on a price freeze that may not carry the control symbols — now measured, and mostly resolved for the matured slice.** R1 (`STEWARD_Q016_exposure.md` §5) measured it rather than assuming it: of the **458** B2 pool rows on the 11 matured nights, **458/458** are carried in `manifest_prices_v001`'s 436-symbol daily universe with ≥ 60 prior daily bars, **455/458** also have a `C_t` and a complete 60-bar forward window, and **88/88** eligible picks get a full 10-nearest-neighbour match. **§8 clause 7 is therefore evaluable today** on the matured slice. **The one named gap: `CTRA`'s daily bars in `p001_daily_split` stop at 2026-05-06 while it keeps appearing as an unpublished candidate through 2026-06-11** — a genuine mid-freeze coverage gap for one symbol, not a maturity or boundary effect — costing the 3 rows above (2026-06-04, 06-05, 06-11) their `C_t`, forward bars and `runup20`. Those rows are **dropped from the control pool and counted, never back-filled or imputed** (§3). This changes no verdict on the matured slice. **The threat that remains is prospective:** clause 7 stays conditional on the R2 successor freezes carrying the same coverage for every future in-window night's candidate symbols, published and unpublished, which cannot be confirmed before those symbols exist — hence the targeted truncated-history scan added to R2's scope (§5). Where a night's control cohort cannot be built, the gate is not evaluable and every endpoint is INCONCLUSIVE rather than CONFIRMED — the conservative reading, chosen so that a missing control cohort cannot quietly upgrade a picks-only gradient into a finding.
9. **Hourly bars exist for published symbols only** (227 in `p001_hourly_raw`), so same-session ordering can never be resolved for the control pool. This costs nothing to the primary, which is order-free by construction; every ordering statistic is descriptive and applies DP-27's adverse-first reading identically in every arm, with the counts and the opposite reading printed.
10. **The 60-prior-bar requirement drops recent listings**, which are disproportionately high-volatility names — precisely the population H-059 is about. The count is printed by symbol and month; the bias runs toward NULL, and any confirmed positive is therefore a lower bound on the true gradient.
11. **Split-corrupted `atr_pct` (PI-003) is never used**; ATR14 and beta60 are computed by the desk from `p001_daily_split`, and `eval.py` fails loudly on a basis disagreement with `p001_daily_raw`.
12. **The metric was chosen on in-sample data — but by the hypothesis, not by this draft.** `±2 ATR / 60` is H-059's own unit and clock (DP-25), quoted in the BACKLOG entry before any sealed night was seen, and §6 records that no sealed two-sidedness figure exists anywhere on the desk. The threshold/clock grid is registered in §4 as sensitivity precisely so that no cell of it can be promoted at the decision pass.
13. **Two-sidedness is not money.** No stop is assumed and no exit is taken (rule 5, DP-02); a round trip is free to a holder who sits through it. A reader who does use stops would experience these paths very differently, and §9 forbids any stop recommendation from this question.
14. **Matching on 3 features may not span selection** (Q006 threat 1). B2 is descriptive here except for the §8 gate, and post-match standardized mean differences are printed with every control-adjusted figure.
