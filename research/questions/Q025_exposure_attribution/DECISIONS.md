## Standing rules proposed

- **An endpoint written as a difference of two matched-control constructions takes 2 × DP-20 for CONFIRMED and 1 × DP-20 for NULL.** The desk already doubles the MPE for a difference of differences (Q007, Q014, Q022). What Q024/Q025 adds is the asymmetry: on a two-sided endpoint a wider MPE band makes **NULL** easier, and a NULL can license something affirmative — here, "the three-feature control was adequate, keep using it". Widening both bands together is not conservative, it just moves the leniency to the other side.
- **A question with two primary endpoints sizes its window on the slower one, and the exposure request measures both.** DP-21's floor is per endpoint, so a window sized on the faster endpoint's rate schedules a shortfall on the other and turns DP-13's extension into the plan. One line, and it only ever moves a date out.
- **An exposure count may report coverage and funnel counts but never post-match balance.** Balance is a pre-registered gate, and a gate measured before lock is a gate pre-certified. This is the counts-only rule applied to the one quantity that looks like a count and is not.
- **One id per question, assigned by registration timestamp, checked before the second draft of a cycle is written.** Two questions in the same autonomous run both took `Q024`; the family correction set is counted by question id, so a collision is not cosmetic.
