## 1. Hypothesis (plain English, one sentence)

**Among published SAS picks that carry a GEX reading on the pick night, those the platform flags with *pin risk* — the biggest gamma wall sitting within a quarter of a percent of the stock's price — go on to touch *both* +2 ATR and −2 ATR from the pick-night close within 60 trading sessions more often than the same night's picks without pin risk, and more often than same-night unpublished candidates of the same price level and volatility.**

This is H-061 as filed: *"GEX pin risk at selection (`gex_context.pin_risk = true`) goes with two-sided paths: pinned picks touch both +2 ATR and −2 ATR within 60 sessions more often — baseline: `pin_risk = false` picks; all candidates with `pin_risk = false`."* The proposed mechanism is dealer hedging: when the largest gamma concentration sits on top of spot, hedging flows pull price back toward that strike from either side, so the path should be a round trip rather than a one-way move.

**Why this question is worth a family slot.** Pin risk is computed on every pick night and then **thrown away**. `gex_alignment` carries weight **0.0** in v1.6 (`volatilx services/super_agent_select_models.py:17`), so the −12 point pin-risk deduction inside `_score_gex_alignment` (`volatilx services/super_agent_select_scoring.py:902-904`) is multiplied by zero and moves no score, no rank and no publication decision. Nothing on the platform tells a subscriber, or Haci, that a pick is sitting on a gamma wall. F3 exists for exactly this: a signal the platform computes and does not use.

**The in-sample read that produced it, quoted so it cannot be re-used as evidence.** EXPLORE_001 §C measured `±2 ATR / 60` two-sidedness at **65% for pinned picks vs 44% without, on 26 picks across 16 nights**, April–May, with GEX context missing for 137 of 280 picks. That is a large gap on a tiny arm in a one-way tape, and it is the reason those nights are excluded from this question entirely (§6). It is also **the single most likely artefact on the desk**: pin risk is mechanically more common in high-priced, densely-struck megacaps, and EXPLORE_001 §D found *low*-ATR picks were the most two-sided (0.54 vs 0.39). §3's matched control and §8's clause 7 exist to separate "pinning does this" from "the kind of stock that gets pinned does this", and a positive raw contrast that does not survive the match is INCONCLUSIVE, not a finding.

**What is *not* claimed here.** Two-sidedness is a statement about the *path*, not about money. No stop is assumed anywhere (rule 5, DP-02); a round trip costs a holder nothing unless he was stopped or exited into it. §9 binds what a confirmed verdict may change.

## 3. Baseline(s) — what this must beat

There is always a baseline, and here there are four, because "pinned picks round-trip often" is meaningless without "and comparable things did not, on the same nights".

- **B1 (primary, and H-061's own first baseline): the same night's published picks with `pin_risk = false`.** Same tape, same night, same publication process, same 60-session clock, same symmetric ±2 ATR band scaled to each pick's own ATR14. This is "Y's rate when X did not happen, in the same regime" in its cleanest available form, and it is the paired contrast the primary estimates (§4).
- **B2 (the matched control, and the gate that decides whether this is a finding): same-night unpublished candidates matched on price level and volatility, all non-pinned.** For each eligible pick (PIN or NOPIN), the **10 nearest same-night unpublished, GEX-computable, `pin_risk = false` `sas_candidates` rows** (the complement of the DP-28 predicate, dark-lane rows included) on **`log C_t` / `atr_pct` / `beta60` / `runup20`** from `prices_daily_split` bars dated ≤ t, standardized by the night's cross-sectional median and MAD, Euclidean distance, with replacement across picks, ties by symbol ascending. This is the Q006 §3 construction **plus `log C_t`**: pin risk is a function of how close the nearest wall sits *in percent*, and strike spacing in percent falls as price rises, so price level is the single largest driver of who gets flagged. **Distance-matching in the rule-5 sense is automatic:** the level is ±2 × the control's *own* ATR14 from the control's *own* close, so every arm carries a target at the identical ATR distance by construction. B2 answers the question B1 cannot — *is this pinning, or is it the kind of stock that gets pinned?* — and it carries a **hard clause in §8**.
- **B3 (H-061's second baseline, the mechanism check): the same PIN − NOPIN contrast computed over the night's *unpublished* GEX-computable candidates.** If dealer hedging near a wall does what the mechanism says, it does it to stocks, not to picks, so the contrast should appear here too. Descriptive, with one sign-consistency clause in §8.
- **B4 (the unconditional rate, descriptive):** the two-sided rate of all eligible published picks pooled, and of the whole GEX-computable candidate universe pooled. This is the "what does a stock do in 60 sessions" number every reported rate must be read against, and it is what stops a 50%-ish two-sided rate sounding like a finding on its own.

## 4. Objective metric (rule 5 — price path, measured the way it is traded)

**Reference point.** `X = C_t`, the pick-night regular-session close from `prices_daily_split`, for picks and matched controls alike (**DP-11**; the proxy for the DP-03(a) after-hours fill). Only the close exists on the control side and rule 5 requires one basis on both sides. It is also the basis on which ATR14 is measured and the basis EXPLORE_001 used for this metric, so the sealed result is comparable to the read that produced the hypothesis. The **next-session-open basis (DP-03(b))** is computed and printed as a named sensitivity.

**The two levels, per row, fixed at the close and never moved:**
`U = X + 2 × ATR14_t` and `D = X − 2 × ATR14_t` (**DP-25** — ±2 ATR is H-061's own unit, taken as written). ATR14 is the desk-standard construction from `p001_daily_split` bars ≤ t (mean true range over the 14 sessions ending at t, `research/reports/explore/scripts/03_build_core.py:71-74`); **the platform's `atr_pct` column is never used** (PI-003; split-corrupted).

**Grading window:** sessions **t+1 .. t+60**, regular-session daily bars only (**DP-25** — 60 sessions is H-061's own clock; §10.2 records that the mechanism's own horizon is shorter, and DECISIONS.md item 4 records why a shorter clock is not promoted to primary).

**The three events, per row:**
- **TOUCH_U = 1** iff some session `s ∈ [t+1, t+60]` has `high_s ≥ U`.
- **TOUCH_D = 1** iff some session `s ∈ [t+1, t+60]` has `low_s ≤ D`.
- **TWO_SIDED = TOUCH_U ∧ TOUCH_D**, the primary event.

**Ordering rules, applied by `eval.py` with no judgment:** (1) a gap through a level is a touch; there is no "already passed at entry" case, since both levels lie 2 ATR from `X` in opposite directions at entry (DP-26 satisfied vacuously). (2) Both levels stay live for all 60 sessions; touching one does not retire the other, and neither is an exit (rule 5, DP-02) — there is no stop in this question. (3) Order of the two touches is **descriptive only**; where both are first touched on the same session, `prices_hourly_raw` orders them for published symbols, and where one hourly bar holds both, or the symbol has no hourly bars (the whole control pool, §10.9), the pair counts **adverse-first** (**DP-27**), with the opposite reading printed beside it. R1 measured hourly coverage at **84/84 (100%)** over t+1..t+60 for matured published picks, so the default is not expected to fire for a published symbol; the rule stays as drafted because coverage for nights not yet run is R2's to establish. None of this touches the primary endpoint, which is a conjunction and is invariant to order — a deliberate choice that makes the primary computable from daily bars alone.

**Primary endpoint (one, pre-specified).** For night `t`, with `mean_PIN[·]` over that night's eligible PIN picks and `mean_NOPIN[·]` over its NOPIN picks:

- **P1 — the pin contrast.** `Δ_t = mean_PIN[TWO_SIDED] − mean_NOPIN[TWO_SIDED]`; estimand `m = mean_t Δ_t` over contributing nights, in **percentage points**. **Two-sided**: H-061 predicts `m > 0`; a result beyond MPE with either sign is a confirmed finding with that sign (§8, §9).

**The control-adjusted contrast (S2) — not a second endpoint, a gate on the first.** For each eligible pick `i`, `excess_i = TWO_SIDED_i − mean(TWO_SIDED over i's 10 matched B2 controls)`. Then `Δ2_t = mean_PIN[excess] − mean_NOPIN[excess]`, and `m2 = mean_t Δ2_t`. This difference-of-differences removes both the selection effect (every arm is measured against its own matched non-picks) and the stock-type effect (the controls share the pick's price level, ATR%, beta and run-up, and are non-pinned by construction). It occupies **no BH slot** and carries **no verdict of its own**; it appears only in §8 clause 7, where it can turn a positive P1 into INCONCLUSIVE but can never create a CONFIRMED on its own.

**Mandatory decomposition, printed with every primary (descriptive, never decides).** A conjunction can rise because an arm moves more in *both* directions or merely more in *one*:
`TWO_SIDED rate`, `TOUCH_U rate`, `TOUCH_D rate`, `P(TOUCH_D | TOUCH_U)`, realized 60-session **range in ATR** (`(max high − min low) / ATR14_t`), and the **forward/backward realized-volatility ratio** — each per arm, with its own CI. The conditional factor is labelled *"conditions on a post-entry path event; descriptive, does not decide"*.

**Secondary, descriptive, never decides:**
- **B3** (the unpublished-candidate pin contrast) and **B4** (the unconditional rates), per §3.
- **The clock and threshold grid**, registered here so it can never be searched at the decision pass: `±2 ATR / 20`, `±2 ATR / 40`, `±1.5 ATR / 20`, `±3 ATR / 60`. The 20- and 40-session cells are the ones the *mechanism* would favour (§10.2). **They are sensitivities, not endpoints. Only `±2 ATR / 60` decides**, whatever the grid shows; a report sentence that leans on a grid cell is out of bounds.
- **The continuous version:** the same endpoint by quintile of `|nearest_wall.dist_pct|`, which is the variable the platform's 0.25% threshold cuts (`volatilx services/gex.py:340-344`). Registered so the knife-edge binary can be read in context; it never decides (DP-25 — the binary is the hypothesis).
- **The Q016 overlap decomposition, mandatory:** the P1 contrast computed **within each `atr_pct` tercile** and **within each `beta60` tercile** (Q016's conditioning variables, same night-relative construction), reported at ≥ 20 contributing nights per cell and SUPPRESSED below. This is the printed read on whether a pin effect is an ATR effect wearing a gamma hat (§10.1, §10.11).
- **The ladder-anchored companion**, so any §9 line can be written on levels the platform prints: first touch of **L3** (`lane_plans.swing_trading.targets[0]`) and of the **printed swing stop** within t+1..t+60, and the share of picks touching both, per arm, on the sub-population carrying a swing lane with its own counts printed.
- The **next-open basis** version; sessions from entry to the first touch of each side; MFE and MAE in ATR from `X`; which side came first (DP-27 as above); `gex_context.regime` (POS_GAMMA / NEG_GAMMA) as a sub-cell — **H-020 is a separate hypothesis and is not answered here**; sub-cells for bull/bear, `best_timeframe` lane, score band (80–85 / 85–90 / 90+), tape stratum, regime label — all reported only at ≥ 20 contributing nights, SUPPRESSED below. **The bull/bear line is marked SUPPRESSED-bearish at lock** (§2, §5: 0 bearish among 137 PIN rows) and is never un-suppressed afterwards; every direction-crossed cell — direction × lane, × band, × tape, × regime, × tercile — prints counts only on the bearish side, no point estimate and no "small n, directionally". **Suppression restricts affirmative reporting only and never removes a cell from §8 clause 6 or clause 8.**
- Close-to-close return from `X` at T+20 and T+60 — **fixed-horizon return is secondary and descriptive by rule 5 and DP-01, and never decides.**

**Quotability.** The primary basis is a 60-session window (**W60**), the quotable basis under rule 12 — but nothing here is quotable before PROSPECTIVELY_CONFIRMED (rule 10, §8). Every grid cell on a 20- or 40-session clock is labelled **NON_QUOTABLE**.

**Inference.** Night-level (rule 6); control rows never add to inferential n.
- **p-value (decides significance):** a **within-night permutation of the pin label** across the night's eligible picks, preserving each night's PIN count, 10,000 permutations, seed 20260913. This is exact under "the pin label is exchangeable within the night" and is **immune to the cross-night dependence that heavily overlapping 60-session windows create** (§10.6) — the reason it, and not a bootstrap p, is primary. A paired sign-flip permutation on the night contrasts is printed alongside.
- **CI (decides the excludes-zero clause):** stationary block bootstrap over the ordered contributing nights, **expected block length 30 sessions** (half the forward window, the Q016 convention, fixed here and not chosen after seeing results), 2,000 resamples. The date-clustered bootstrap CI is printed alongside and is expected to be narrower; **the block-bootstrap interval decides.**
- Every estimate prints: n(contributing nights), n(contributing nights dated after the lock commit), n(eligible picks per arm), n(TWO_SIDED), n(TOUCH_U), n(TOUCH_D), n(NOT_COMPUTABLE, by clause), n(control rows and control-set sizes), n(ungradeable), n(immature), n(excluded, by reason), and post-match standardized mean differences for B2 — including, as balance diagnostics that are **reported but not matched on**, `quality.contracts_used` and the nearest-wall strike spacing in percent (§10.13). **`|SMD| > 0.25` on either diagnostic is declared a large residual imbalance and is printed beside the verdict, in the report and in the LEDGER line.** This is a **disclosure, not a gate**: no verdict turns on it, and it neither creates nor blocks a CONFIRMED. R1's matured-window read already points this way — PIN picks carry a higher and tighter `contracts_used` (median 48) than NOPIN picks (47.5) and the B2 pool (43) — and the SMD itself is `eval.py`'s to compute at the decision pass.

## 8. Decision rule (numeric, written before unsealing)

`m` is in **percentage points of TWO_SIDED rate** (PIN minus NOPIN, paired within night), over contributing nights.

**MPE: +5.0 pp** (DP-20, DP-44 — the standing number for a control-adjusted or within-night touch-rate endpoint). No uplift is proposed: this is a within-night paired contrast on one tape, the Q011 / Q015 / Q016 structure. A gradient smaller than 5 pp would not change how a position is structured. The same 5.0 pp applies to the S2 gate, in the same units.

The test is **two-sided**; the verdict carries its sign.

- **HISTORICALLY_CONFIRMED** requires **all** of:
  1. **≥ 80 contributing nights** and **≥ 30 contributing nights dated after the lock commit** (DP-21, DP-24), **and ≥ 20 contributing nights for any sub-cell that is reported** — a sub-cell below floor is **SUPPRESSED** (counts only) and does **not by itself** make the endpoint INCONCLUSIVE. The **bearish PIN sub-cell is SUPPRESSED at lock** (§2, §4, §5) along with every direction-crossed cell on the bearish side. **Suppression — at lock or at the decision pass — restricts affirmative reporting only; it never removes a cell from clause 6 or clause 8 below**, which apply to whatever the decision pass measures, whatever was projected at lock, and are never a route to an easier CONFIRMED;
  2. `|m| > 5.0 pp`;
  3. the **block-bootstrap** 95% CI (§4) excludes 0;
  4. BH **q ≤ 0.10** in the F3 correction **and** in the F7 companion correction (§7), computed on the within-night permutation p, with **the larger of the two q-values quoted** wherever a q is reported;
  5. the point estimate has the **same sign in both halves**, and neither half is beyond MPE in the opposite sign;
  6. no tape stratum with ≥ 20 contributing nights is beyond MPE in the opposite sign;
  7. **the control-adjusted contrast `m2` (S2, §4) exceeds MPE in the same sign as `m`** — the "is this pinning, or is it the kind of stock that gets pinned" gate. If `m2` is below MPE, or of the opposite sign, the verdict is **INCONCLUSIVE**, whatever P1 says: the raw contrast is then attributable to price level, ATR%, beta or run-up, which the matched control holds fixed and the raw baseline does not. **If S2 cannot be computed** for a night because the price freeze does not carry the control symbols' 60 prior and 60 forward bars, or because a night's matched pool cannot be built at all (§10.10), the clause is **not evaluable and the verdict is INCONCLUSIVE**, never CONFIRMED on B1 alone.   8. **B3 (the unpublished-candidate pin contrast) is not beyond MPE in the opposite sign.** A pin effect among picks that reverses among candidates of the same night is a finding about selection or about noise, not about gamma, and it is reported as INCONCLUSIVE.

## 10. Known threats to validity (registrar's own list)

1. **Pin risk is largely a statement about the option chain, not about the stock — the dominant confound.** `_pin_risk` is true when the nearest of the five largest gamma walls sits within **0.25%** of spot (`volatilx services/gex.py:340-344`, `:25-31`). Strike spacing in percent falls as price rises and as a chain gets denser, so pinned names skew toward high-priced, heavily-optioned megacaps — which are also comparatively low-ATR, and EXPLORE_001 §D found *low*-ATR picks the most two-sided in ATR units. The whole of §8 clause 7 exists for this: the matched control holds `log C_t`, `atr_pct`, `beta60` and `runup20` fixed and is itself non-pinned, and a raw effect that does not survive it is INCONCLUSIVE.
2. **The mechanism's horizon is shorter than the registered clock.** The chain used for the gamma picture spans at most 60 **calendar** days to expiry (`volatilx services/gex.py:25-31`), and pinning bites hardest into the nearest expiry — days, not months — while the endpoint runs 60 **sessions**. The clock is H-061's own and DP-25 forbids re-uniting it; the 20- and 40-session cells are registered in §4 as sensitivities precisely so they can be read without being promoted. The likeliest honest outcome of this mismatch is NULL, which is the acceptable direction of error.
3. **`pin_risk = false` conflates "not pinned" with "not measurable".** The degraded snapshot path writes `regime="UNKNOWN"`, empty walls and `pin_risk=False` when spot cannot be resolved (`volatilx services/gex.py:676-698`), and `_pin_risk` also returns `False` on an empty wall list after the open-interest filter. The §2 computability filter removes both, at the cost of shrinking the baseline arm; R1 measured the cost at **16 / 550 rows (2.9%)**, 3 of them carrying the degraded-snapshot fingerprint exactly. The removed counts are printed by failing clause, and any night that loses its whole NOPIN arm becomes non-contributing rather than quietly one-armed.
4. **The population is a sub-population, and conditioning on GEX coverage is not neutral.** Roughly half of picks carried GEX context in-sample (143 / 280) — 97.1% are computable in the sealed window, so the conditioning is milder than feared but not absent — and having context adds +0.75 to the "short" timeframe bucket (`volatilx services/super_agent_select_scoring.py:670-672`), so covered picks skew short-lane and toward names with liquid chains. Everything here therefore applies to **GEX-covered picks only** and §9's guide line must say so. Both arms are covered, so the *contrast* is not biased by coverage; the *generalization* is.
5. **The PIN arm is thin — one to six picks on a contributing night.** Night-level means over a one-row arm are noisy, and the noise is symmetric, so it widens the CI and pushes toward INCONCLUSIVE rather than toward a false positive. The permutation test is exact under label exchangeability regardless of arm size; the block bootstrap is the fragile half.
6. **Overlapping 60-session windows are the worst dependence structure the desk registers.** Adjacent nights share ~98% of their outcome sessions, so night counts overstate independent information. Two pre-specified protections: the within-night permutation p, exact under label exchangeability and free of cross-night independence assumptions, and a 30-session block bootstrap for the CI, fixed here and not chosen after seeing results.
7. **The GEX table is upserted in place.** `gex_symbol_daily` rows are overwritten by any later run for the same date (`volatilx services/gex.py:605-629`, `:823-840`), so the table is not point-in-time. The feature is taken from the candidate row's 16:05 `context_json` snapshot instead, and the disagreement rate between the two is measured and reported (§6). **Measured at R1: 527 / 537 = 98.1% agreement, 10 disagreements, every one at `|dist_pct|` 0.0005–0.0071 — the knife edge — and 12 published rows with no `gex_symbol_daily` row at all (an audit gap, not an exclusion).** A high disagreement rate would not invalidate the endpoint — the snapshot is what SAS held — but it is a data-quality fact the desk should know, and the measured pattern is consistent with an in-place upsert seeing a shifted wall list rather than with a logic change (R1 item 9 found no in-window commit).
8. **Regime confound, and the label is assigned before the window that matters.** A 60-session window from a June 2026 night ends in September; from an April 2027 night it ends in July 2027. The stratum label is legal only at 16:05 ET on the pick night (rule 14), so it describes the tape *entering* the window. The halves and the tape strata are the only protection; if the whole window turns out to be one tape, rule 7 bites and the honest verdict is INCONCLUSIVE.
9. **Hourly bars exist for published symbols only**, so same-session ordering can never be resolved for the control pool. This costs nothing to the primary, which is order-free by construction; every ordering statistic is descriptive and applies DP-27's adverse-first reading identically in every arm. R1 measured 100% hourly coverage for matured published picks, so the default is not expected to fire on the published side.
10. **The matched control depends on a price freeze that carries the unpublished symbols.** R1 item (7) measured this for the matured slice: 178/178 pool symbols carried, 372/375 (99.2%) rows with both 60 prior and 60 forward bars, 84/84 picks matched in full. A **whole-history scan of all 431 candidate symbols** found **CTRA is the only symbol with the truncated-history signature** (daily bars stop 2026-05-06 while it keeps appearing in `sas_candidates` through 2026-09-10) and **no candidate symbol missing from the 436-symbol freeze entirely**; CTRA costs 3 of 375 B2-pool rows here and cost Q016 3 of 458. Those rows are **dropped and counted, never imputed** (§8 clause 7), and R3 records the fact in `research/data/DATA_NOTES.md` so the next question cites it instead of re-running the scan. The check is not retired: R2 item (4) re-runs the targeted scan before either successor freeze is called complete, because a *new* CTRA can appear at any time, and where a night's matched pool cannot be built at all, §8 clause 7 is unevaluable and the verdict is INCONCLUSIVE.
11. **Q016 tests the same endpoint on a correlated conditioning variable.** If pinned names are systematically low-ATR, a Q021 positive and Q016's P2 negative may be one fact. The within-ATR-tercile and within-beta-tercile decompositions are mandatory (§4), the F7 companion correction is required with the larger q quoted (§7), and §9 ships nothing that the decomposition contradicts.
12. **The 0.25% threshold is the platform's, and it is a knife edge.** A pick at 0.26% is in the baseline arm and a pick at 0.24% is in the treatment arm, and the boundary was set by a config default, not by evidence. R1 confirms the mechanics exactly — 137 / 534 computable rows (25.7%) sit at or inside 0.0025 and that is the PIN arm to the row — and the mass thins smoothly across the boundary with no second cluster, so the cut is arbitrary rather than natural. The continuous `|nearest_wall.dist_pct|` version is printed for context and never decides (DP-25 — the binary is the hypothesis).
13. **Matching on four features may not span what makes a name pinnable.** Option-chain size and strike spacing are the mechanism's own variables and are *not* matched on, because doing so would thin the pools further and can make §8 clause 7 unevaluable, which is infeasibility rather than strictness. They are reported as post-match standardized mean differences (`quality.contracts_used`, nearest-wall percent spacing) with every control-adjusted figure, and **`|SMD| > 0.25` on either diagnostic is declared a large residual imbalance and disclosed beside the verdict** (§4, §8). The threshold is the standard matching convention and is fixed here so the report cannot re-argue what "large" means after seeing it; it creates **no gate** and no verdict turns on it. R1's matured-window read already shows the direction of the imbalance: PIN picks carry a higher, tighter `contracts_used` (median 48, IQR 45–50) than NOPIN picks (47.5, 40.5–50) and the B2 pool (43, 34–49).
14. **Two-sidedness is not money.** No stop is assumed and no exit is taken (rule 5, DP-02); a round trip is free to a holder who sits through it. A reader who does use stops would experience these paths very differently, and §9 forbids any stop, DTE or option-structure recommendation from this question.
15. **The window is long, and the ceiling risk this threat named has been measured away — leaving the opposite exposure.** The initial decision date sits **≈ 10.9 months** after lock, inside DP-43's 12-month ceiling. The run-rate risk is resolved: §5's 0.32/session DEFERRED trigger was evaluated at `record` and did **not** fire, because R1 measured **0.868–0.909 contributing nights per session** against a 0.35 plan, and the window projects **≈ 198 contributing nights against a floor of 80**. What remains is the mirror-image cost: the desk waits roughly six months longer than the measured rate requires — the Steward's projected floor dates (≈ 2027-01-25 for 80 nights, ≈ 2027-02-08 for 30 post-lock) are **context only and move nothing**, because DP-45 forbids taking the option that reaches a decision sooner and DP-43 forbids shortening a registered window. The residual risk is a collapse in the rate mid-window, for which the DP-13 extension is held in reserve; there is no second extension, and a gate still short at 2027-09-20 sends this question to DEFERRED rather than to a verdict.
