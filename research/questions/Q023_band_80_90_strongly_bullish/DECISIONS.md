## Standing rules proposed

- **A knowledge-time test on a same-day-written table is a timestamp comparison against the consumer that used it, not a date comparison.** "`created_at` falls on its own `trading_date`" passes a row written at 19:00 ET; the honest test is `created_at ≤ the night's own run`. `market_regime_daily` is the first table where the desk has needed this, and it will not be the last (any 16:05 ET / lag 0 table with a same-evening write window).
- **When a conditioning variable is produced by a smoothing filter, the inference unit is the episode, and the episode is defined on the label series — not on the sampled nights.** Defining it on contributing nights lets an exclusion manufacture an extra independent draw. Q023 is the first question whose arm comes from a hysteresis filter; the rule belongs beside DP-26 rather than being re-derived.
- **A gate that exists in §8 exists in the exposure request.** Q023's draft gated on ≥ 5 episodes per arm and then sized its window on nights alone; the DEFERRED test must carry a limb for **every** floor the decision rule names, or the question locks on an arithmetic that cannot reach its own verdict.
- **DP-12's "total effect is descriptive" applies to night-level tape conditioning, not only to a pick's own path.** Where a primary endpoint has no control inside it because the arm *is* the tape, it carries its control-adjusted companion as a binding same-sign clause and licenses no rule on its own.

Four more, added at `record` (all from item 13 and the suppression list; each would generalise beyond Q023, and none is Haci's word):

- **A window is sized in the unit in which the rarer arm's nights actually arrive.** Where the conditioning variable is episodic — a hysteresis label, a regime, anything that arrives in blocks — a flat per-session night rate prices a floor that cannot be reached in fractions. The floor is converted to **whole episodes** at the measured episodes-per-session and nights-per-episode, and rounded up. Q023's floor B costs 114 sessions that way and 100 the flat way; the flat way expects the DP-13 extension to fire. This is the general form of the answer to "the rate is fine on average but zero lately".
- **DP-13 is the fallback for a measured shortfall, never the plan.** A window whose floors are projected met *only with the extension* is not a window — it is a 50/50 bet that a second freeze will be needed. If the projection at the primary window end does not clear every floor with margin, the primary window is extended before lock, not after.
- **A decision date that lands on a market holiday moves out to the next Monday.** DP-43's "first Monday on or after" has no holiday clause; moving out is always permitted and moving in never is, so the rule resolves itself. Q023's 2027-05-31 is Memorial Day, hence 2027-06-07.
- **When the rarer arm barely clears its own floor, the sub-cell suppression list follows by arithmetic, not case by case.** An arm at 22.8 projected nights against a 20-night floor cannot support any split of itself, so every sub-cell that splits it is suppressed at lock in one stroke — and the clauses that *block* (halves, bull-only, the alternative label, the control-adjusted companion) are named as exempt in the same breath, so suppression never quietly removes a guard.
