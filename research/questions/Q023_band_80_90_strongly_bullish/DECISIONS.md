## Standing rules proposed

- **A knowledge-time test on a same-day-written table is a timestamp comparison against the consumer that used it, not a date comparison.** "`created_at` falls on its own `trading_date`" passes a row written at 19:00 ET; the honest test is `created_at ≤ the night's own run`. `market_regime_daily` is the first table where the desk has needed this, and it will not be the last (any 16:05 ET / lag 0 table with a same-evening write window).
- **When a conditioning variable is produced by a smoothing filter, the inference unit is the episode, and the episode is defined on the label series — not on the sampled nights.** Defining it on contributing nights lets an exclusion manufacture an extra independent draw. Q023 is the first question whose arm comes from a hysteresis filter; the rule belongs beside DP-26 rather than being re-derived.
- **A gate that exists in §8 exists in the exposure request.** Q023's draft gated on ≥ 5 episodes per arm and then sized its window on nights alone; the DEFERRED test must carry a limb for **every** floor the decision rule names, or the question locks on an arithmetic that cannot reach its own verdict.
- **DP-12's "total effect is descriptive" applies to night-level tape conditioning, not only to a pick's own path.** Where a primary endpoint has no control inside it because the arm *is* the tape, it carries its control-adjusted companion as a binding same-sign clause and licenses no rule on its own.
