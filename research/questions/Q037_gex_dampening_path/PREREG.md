## 1. Hypothesis (plain English, one sentence)

**When the SAS report warns a subscriber that this pick's gamma backdrop should dampen follow-through — the stock is in a positive-gamma regime *and* a gamma wall sits between the entry price and the first target of the lane — the pick reaches that target less often than picks with neither condition on the same night, after both are measured against same-night unpublished candidates carrying targets at the identical ATR distance.**

This is **H-091** as filed (INBOX 2026-09-14). The platform prints the warning today and acts on it not at all: `gex_alignment` carries weight **0.0** in v1.6 (`volatilx services/super_agent_select_models.py:14-22`) with a missing-data penalty of **0.0** (`:25-34`), so the −8 "Positive gamma can dampen directional follow-through" deduction (`services/super_agent_select_scoring.py:898-900`) and the −10 "Nearby gamma wall may interfere with follow-through" deduction (`:906-912`) are multiplied by zero and move no score, no rank and no publication decision. The words still reach the subscriber: the public risk list harvests any reason string containing `dampen` or `interfere` (`services/super_agent_select_public.py:17-27`), and the synthesis layer restates them (SWKS, 2026-09-11 — "positive gamma may dampen follow-through", "nearby call wall resistance"). **If the warning is true it is a free improvement nobody is using; if it is false the platform is printing a caution it cannot support.** Either verdict is worth an F3 slot.

**What is *not* claimed.** This is a statement about first touch of a printed target, not about money. No stop is assumed and no exit is taken (rule 5, DP-02). It says nothing about whether the *score* should change — that is Q029's subject (§7), and §9 forbids this question from licensing a weight change.

### 2.5 The arms (assigned at 16:05 ET, frozen before any forward bar is loaded)

Per pick `i` with entry `X_i` (§4) and that endpoint's target `L_i`, **travel side** = up for `dominant_direction = bullish`, down for `bearish`:

- **WALL_IN_PATH** = there exists a wall `w ∈ gex_context.top_walls` on the travel side whose `strike` lies between `X_i` and `L_i` **inclusive of the endpoints** (a strike exactly at `X_i` or at `L_i` counts as in-path — the reading less favourable to the hypothesis, DP-27's tie principle, since it dilutes the dampened arm toward the population mean). Whether `w` must additionally be side-matched (`side = "call_wall"` for bullish travel, `"put_wall"` for bearish) is **§11 decision 1**; the alternative reading is a registered secondary either way.
- **D (dampened)** = GEX-computable ∧ `regime = "POS_GAMMA"` ∧ WALL_IN_PATH.
- **C (clear, the baseline arm)** = GEX-computable ∧ `regime = "NEG_GAMMA"` ∧ ¬WALL_IN_PATH.
- **M (mixed)** = GEX-computable ∧ exactly one of the two conditions. **M is reported with counts and its own excess rate and never enters the primary contrast** (§4).

**Arm membership is endpoint-specific by construction**, because `L_i` differs between E1 and E2: a pick may be D for E2 and C or M for E1. This is H-091 as filed ("the lane's first target"), and every count, floor and contributing-night test below is computed **separately per endpoint**.

## 3. Baseline(s) — what this must beat

- **B1 (H-091's own baseline, and the paired contrast the primary estimates): the same night's published C-arm picks** — picks with neither dampening condition, same tape, same publication process, same clock, each target measured against its own committed level. This is "Y's rate when X did not happen, in the same regime".
- **B2 (the rule-5 distance-matched control, applied to *each* arm separately, and the reason the primary is a difference in *excess*): same-night unpublished candidates with a target at the identical ATR distance.** For each eligible pick `i`, take the **10 nearest same-night unpublished, GEX-computable `sas_candidates` rows** (the complement of the DP-28 predicate, dark-lane rows included) on **`log C_t` / `atr_pct` / `beta60` / `runup20`** from `prices_daily_split` bars ≤ t, standardized by the night's cross-sectional median and MAD, Euclidean distance, with replacement across picks, ties by symbol ascending (the Q006 §3 construction plus `log C_t`, as in Q021 §3). Each control carries a synthetic target at the **same ATR distance** `k_i = (L_i − X_i) / ATR14_t(i)` from **its own** close, in the **same direction**, graded over the **same 20 sessions**. Controls are **not** matched on GEX state: the control's job is to remove the "how far is this target, on this kind of stock, on this night" component, so that the D−C contrast is not a comparison of distances.
- **B3 (mechanism check, descriptive): the same D − C contrast computed over the night's *unpublished* GEX-computable candidates**, with each candidate assigned a synthetic target at the night's median published `k` for its lane. If dealer hedging near a wall does what the warning says, it does it to stocks, not to picks. One sign-consistency clause in §8.
- **B4 (the unconditional rate, descriptive):** the raw L1 and L3 touch rates of all eligible published picks pooled, and of the GEX-computable candidate universe pooled. Every reported rate is read against this. It is what stops a high L1 rate sounding like a finding: the desk has already recorded that **L1 is usually touched on day 1 (median step 1)** on picks (`research/reports/weekly/2026-09-10.md`), which is exactly why E1 carries the ceiling threat in §10.

## 4. Objective metric (rule 5 — the price path, measured the way it is traded)

**Entry basis.** `X_i = C_t`, the pick-night regular-session close from `prices_daily_split`, for picks and matched controls alike (**DP-11**, applied as in Q021 §4: only the close exists on the control side and rule 5 requires one basis on both sides; it is the proxy for the DP-03(a) after-hours fill). The **next-session-open basis (DP-03(b))** is computed and printed as a named sensitivity that never decides. A target already passed at `X_i` is not a hit and the row is excluded (§2.6).

**The two events, per row, per endpoint.** With the endpoint's window `s ∈ [t+1, t+20]` and daily regular-session bars:

- **E1:** `TOUCH_L1 = 1` iff some `s` has `high_s ≥ L1` (bullish) or `low_s ≤ L1` (bearish).
- **E2:** `TOUCH_L3 = 1` iff some `s` has `high_s ≥ L3` (bullish) or `low_s ≤ L3` (bearish).

A gap through a level is a touch (DP-26). Touching a level retires nothing and is never an exit (rule 5, DP-02); there is no stop in this question. Controls are graded identically against their own synthetic level.

**Excess, per pick.** `excess_i = TOUCH_i − mean(TOUCH over i's 10 matched B2 controls)`.

**Primary endpoints (two, pre-specified, one per level).** For night `t`:

- **P1 (E1) — `Δ1_t = mean_D[excess] − mean_C[excess]`** over that night's eligible **L1** arms; estimand **`m1 = mean_t Δ1_t`** over E1-contributing nights, in **percentage points**.
- **P2 (E2) — `Δ3_t = mean_D[excess] − mean_C[excess]`** over that night's eligible **L3** arms; estimand **`m3 = mean_t Δ3_t`**, same units.

Both are **two-sided**; H-091 predicts `m < 0` (dampened picks touch *less*), and a result beyond MPE with either sign is a confirmed finding carrying that sign (§8, §9).

**Registered secondaries — printed always, deciding never.** (They are registered here so that none of them can be searched for at the decision pass.)

1. **The complement contrast:** `mean_D[excess] − mean_{¬D}[excess]`, where `¬D` pools C and M. This is the larger-n version of the same idea and is the pre-registered fallback *report*, not a fallback *verdict* (§11 decision 3).
2. **The two marginal decompositions:** POS_GAMMA − NEG_GAMMA ignoring walls, and WALL_IN_PATH − ¬WALL_IN_PATH ignoring regime. A conjunction that is entirely one of its two components is a different finding and must be visible.
3. **The alternative wall reading** (whichever of §11 decision 1's options is not primary), computed on the same rows.
4. **The M arm's own excess rate**, with counts.
5. **B3** and **B4** (§3).
6. **E2 on the platform's 40-session swing window** (§2.4), and E1/E2 on the next-open basis.
7. **Speed (DP-08):** sessions from entry to first touch, per arm, with the median and the survival curve; and the **L1-within-2-sessions** form (the Q008 fast-start clock). Sessions-to-touch differences are **descriptive and carry no MPE** (DP-44).
8. **Adverse excursion and the counter-direction levels:** MAE in ATR from `X_i` over the window, and first touch of the lane's printed stop, per arm (reported, never used as an exit — DP-02).
9. **The committed scale-out companion:** for picks carrying a full lane plan, the realized result of the committed L1–L6 schedule (`volatilx scripts/generate_sas_trading_guide.py` BAND_EXITS as restated in `services/sas_conviction_card.py:197-219`), per arm, so the path rates are never read as money on their own (rule 5).
10. **Sub-cells** — direction, `best_timeframe` lane, score band (80–85 / 85–90 / 90+), tape stratum, regime label, `k` (target distance in ATR) tercile, `atr_pct` tercile, `|nearest_wall.dist_pct|` quintile, and **`synthesis_status`** (the descriptive split that carries the LLM `risk_flags` companion, below). Reported only at **≥ 20 contributing nights**; **SUPPRESSED** below, counts only, never "small n, directionally".
11. **Close-to-close return** from `X_i` at T+20 — **fixed-horizon return is descriptive and never decides** (rule 5, DP-01).

**The LLM `risk_flags` companion is descriptive by construction and can never decide.** The free-text risk strings are written by the GPT synthesis (`volatilx ai_agents/super_agent_select_agent.py:177`, `:628`) and are **empty whenever synthesis falls back** (`:440`), so absence of a flag is confounded with synthesis failure. The arms are therefore defined from the deterministic `gex_context` fields only (§2.5). The companion — "did the printed report actually carry a gamma caution, and does the endpoint differ when it did?" — is reported **split by `synthesis_status`**, with the fallback stratum shown separately, and is labelled *descriptive; absence of a flag is not evidence of no warning*.

**The matched-picks gate (S2) — not a third endpoint, a gate on the first two.** The dampening state is not randomly assigned: POS_GAMMA concentrates in large, densely-struck, slow names, and WALL_IN_PATH is partly a statement about how wide the entry-to-target band is in percent. B2 already nets each arm against its own distance-matched neighbourhood, which removes most of this; S2 removes the rest. For each night, match each **D** pick to the nearest eligible **C** pick of the same night and direction on **`log C_t` / `atr_pct` / `beta60` / `runup20` / `k` / `(L−X)/X`** (same standardization as B2, 1:1 without replacement, ties by symbol ascending, unmatched picks dropped and counted), and recompute the same difference in excess on the matched pairs: `m1_S2`, `m3_S2`. S2 occupies **no BH slot** and carries **no verdict of its own**; it appears only in §8 clause 6, where it can turn a positive primary into INCONCLUSIVE and can never create a CONFIRMED.

**Quotability.** Both primaries run on a 20-session window: **NON_QUOTABLE** under rule 12, in the report, the LEDGER line and any brief. The 40-session and W60 companions are likewise not quotable before PROSPECTIVELY_CONFIRMED (rule 10).

**Inference.** The unit is the trading night (rule 6); control rows never add to inferential n.

- **p-value (decides significance):** a **within-night permutation of the arm label** across the night's eligible D ∪ C picks, preserving each night's D count, **10,000 permutations, seed 20260914**, per endpoint. Exact under "the dampening label is exchangeable within the night", and free of the cross-night dependence that overlapping 20-session windows create.
- **CI (decides the excludes-zero clause):** a **stationary block bootstrap** over the ordered contributing nights, **expected block length 10 sessions** (half the forward window — the Q016/Q021 convention, fixed here and not chosen after seeing results), 2,000 resamples, seed 20260914.
- **Episode-clustered CI (DP-51, mandatory):** an **episode** is one symbol's run of selections with gaps of ≤ 10 sessions between successive selections (the TI-003 / Q003 window); the bootstrap resamples **episodes and nights jointly**, 2,000 resamples, same seed. A primary that clears the MPE on the block/date-clustered CI and not on the episode-clustered CI is **INCONCLUSIVE, never CONFIRMED** (§8). The plain date-clustered CI is printed beside both.
- **Every estimate prints:** n(contributing nights, per endpoint), n(contributing nights dated after the lock commit), n(eligible picks per arm per endpoint), n(touches), n(M), n(NOT_COMPUTABLE by clause), n(no-lane-target), n(price-scale), n(passed-at-entry), n(spot-basis guard), n(ungradeable), n(immature), n(control rows and control-set sizes), n(S2 pairs and unmatched picks), and post-match standardized mean differences for B2 and S2 — including, as **reported-not-matched** diagnostics, `quality.contracts_used` and nearest-wall percent spacing. **`|SMD| > 0.25` on any diagnostic is declared a large residual imbalance and printed beside the verdict** — a disclosure, not a gate.

### 5.4 Precision and power at the MPE (DP-57 — stated plainly, because the floor does not guarantee it)

The per-night contrast is a difference of two thin-arm means of a bounded excess. Taking the per-pick excess SD at ≈ 0.45 and arms of 1–3 picks, the per-night `Δ_t` SD is ≈ 0.45 in rate units, so at **N = 80** contributing nights `SE(m) ≈ 5.0 pp` and at **N = 103** `SE(m) ≈ 4.4 pp`. Consequences, registered before the fact:

- **Minimum detectable effect at 80% power, two-sided α = 0.05: ≈ 14 pp at N = 80, ≈ 12 pp at N = 103.** The design is powered for a *large* dampening effect, not for one at the MPE.
- **An effect of exactly the MPE (5 pp) will usually return INCONCLUSIVE**, not CONFIRMED and not NULL. Detecting 5 pp at 80% power would need ≈ 630 contributing nights, i.e. roughly five years of this platform's slate — outside DP-43's 12-month ceiling by an order of magnitude, so it is not registrable and the honest statement is this paragraph rather than a longer window.
- **NULL as written in §8 is an equivalence claim** and needs a CI half-width ≤ 5 pp, i.e. ≈ 320 contributing nights. **NULL is therefore unlikely to be reachable on this window**, and that is stated in §8 rather than being discovered afterwards.
- **Dependence** widens all of this: adjacent nights' 20-session windows overlap by up to 95%, and the same symbol is re-selected across nights. The block and episode bootstraps (§4) price both; the permutation p is immune to the first.
- **Multiplicity:** two positively-correlated primaries within the question and three primaries in F3 (§7); BH is conservative under positive dependence.
- **Strata:** a sub-cell needs 20 contributing nights; at the central rate a cell clears that by holding ≈ 20% of contributing nights, so the direction cells (bearish is ≈ 2.5% of published rows — 14 of 550 in `STEWARD_Q017_exposure.md`) are **expected to be SUPPRESSED at the decision pass**, and every direction-crossed cell prints counts only on the bearish side.

This question is registered with that ceiling visible: the platform prints the warning as a material caution, so the effect worth finding is a large one, and an INCONCLUSIVE on a 5–12 pp effect is the correct answer for this n rather than a failure of the design.

## 8. Decision rule (numeric, written before unsealing)

`m1` and `m3` are in **percentage points of excess touch rate** (D minus C, paired within night), over that endpoint's contributing nights. The test is **two-sided** and the verdict carries its sign.

**MPE: 5.0 pp** on each primary (**DP-20**, **DP-44** — the standing number for a control-adjusted touch-rate endpoint). No uplift is proposed: this is a within-night paired contrast on one tape, the Q011 / Q015 / Q016 / Q021 structure. The same 5.0 pp applies to the S2 gate, in the same units. **"Positive but below MPE" is INCONCLUSIVE** (rule 6), and §5.4 records that an effect near the MPE will usually land there.

**Each endpoint is judged on its own; the question's headline verdict is the stronger-evidenced primary, with both reported and both q-values quoted.**

- **HISTORICALLY_CONFIRMED** on an endpoint requires **all** of:
  1. **≥ 80 contributing nights** for that endpoint and **≥ 30 contributing nights dated after the lock commit** (DP-21, DP-24), and **≥ 20 contributing nights for any sub-cell that is reported** — a sub-cell below floor is **SUPPRESSED** (counts only) and does not by itself make the endpoint INCONCLUSIVE;
  2. `|m| > 5.0 pp`;
  3. the **block-bootstrap 95% CI excludes 0** *and* the **episode-clustered 95% CI excludes 0** (DP-51). **An endpoint clearing the MPE on the block/date-clustered CI but not on the episode-clustered CI is INCONCLUSIVE, never CONFIRMED**;
  4. BH **q ≤ 0.10** across F3's 3 primaries (§7), computed on the within-night permutation p;
  5. the point estimate has the **same sign in both halves**, and neither half is beyond MPE in the opposite sign; and no tape stratum with ≥ 20 contributing nights is beyond MPE in the opposite sign;
  6. **the matched-picks gate S2 (§4) exceeds MPE in the same sign** — the "is this the gamma state, or the kind of stock that has it" gate. If `m_S2` is below MPE, or of the opposite sign, the verdict is **INCONCLUSIVE** whatever the primary says. **If S2 cannot be computed** — a night's D picks obtain no same-night same-direction C match, or the price freeze does not carry the control symbols' bars — the clause is **not evaluable and the verdict is INCONCLUSIVE**, never CONFIRMED on B1 alone. Individual rows lost to a truncated history are dropped and counted, never imputed, and do not by themselves make the clause unevaluable. **Feasibility is R1 item 5 and is checked again at the decision pass.**
  7. **B3 (the unpublished-candidate D − C contrast) is not beyond MPE in the opposite sign.** A dampening effect among picks that reverses among the same night's candidates is a finding about selection or about noise, not about gamma.
- **NULL:** floors met **and** both 95% CIs lie entirely inside **(−5.0 pp, +5.0 pp)** — an equivalence rule, not "the CI includes zero". This rules out the claimed meaningful effect with the precision the desk requires, and it is the only reading under which the platform could be told its printed warning has no measurable path consequence. **§5.4 records that this needs a CI half-width ≤ 5 pp (≈ 320 contributing nights) and is unlikely to be reachable on this window**; that is registered here rather than discovered at the decision pass, and the floors are not lowered to manufacture it.
- **INCONCLUSIVE:** anything else — halves disagreeing in sign; a tape stratum beyond MPE in the opposite sign; clause 6 failing or unevaluable; clause 7 failing; `0 < |m| ≤ 5.0 pp` with a CI excluding 0 ("real but below MPE", rule 6); a CI including 0 with `|m| ≥ 5.0 pp`; or the two CIs disagreeing (clause 3). **A SUPPRESSED sub-cell is not by itself INCONCLUSIVE**; a *reported* sub-cell below floor is, and must not happen. **A gate shortfall is not INCONCLUSIVE either** — it fires §5.5's single DP-13 extension, then DEFERRED.
- **PROSPECTIVELY_CONFIRMED:** reachable from this run by design (§5.5) — it requires **≥ 30 contributing nights dated after this file's lock commit** (DP-24, DP-21), frozen in the successor manifests, never inspected earlier, reproducing the sign under the unmodified `eval.py`. The clause does not weaken. No subscriber-facing statement before that (rule 10), and nothing here is quotable in any case before the W60 exposure policy is satisfied (rule 12, §4).
- **Fixed look: 2027-06-07, deciding at α = 0.05.** **Interim look: none** (§5.5, §11 decision 2). **No unregistered interim outcome look, ever.** Any future sequential design for this question would have to pre-register boundaries, dependence assumptions and family correction before any prospective outcome is inspected; none is registered here, and repeated ordinary CI checks are not a sequential design.
- **What a confirmed endpoint does and does not mean (binding on the report).** A CONFIRMED-negative `m3` means **"on the same night, picks in a positive-gamma regime with a wall between the entry close and the swing L3 reached L3 within 20 sessions less often than picks with neither condition, after each arm was measured against same-night unpublished candidates carrying targets at the identical ATR distance, and the gap survived matching D picks to C picks on price level, ATR%, beta, run-up and target distance."** It does **not** mean the pick loses money, that the wall caused the shortfall, or that the score should change. A confirmed **positive** means dampened picks reached the target *more* often — the platform's warning is backwards — and is reported with its sign and the same care.

## 10. Known threats to validity (registrar's own list)

1. **The C arm is the scarce one, and that is the question's main feasibility risk.** The nearest wall sits within 0.53% of spot at the median and 1.07% at the 75th percentile (`STEWARD_Q017_exposure.md` item 2), so WALL_IN_PATH is expected to be common and "neither condition" rare — the D arm may be large while C starves. Nothing about the arm shares is measured today. §5.5's 0.27/session trigger exists for this and sends the question to DEFERRED rather than to a switched baseline; §11 decision 3 records why the complement is a reported secondary and not a fallback verdict.
2. **Power, stated in §5.4 and repeated here because it is the largest honest limitation.** The MDE at 80% power is ≈ 12–14 pp; the MPE is 5 pp; NULL as an equivalence claim is probably unreachable on this window. A true dampening effect of 5–12 pp will most often return INCONCLUSIVE.
3. **The L1 ceiling.** The desk's weekly report records that **L1 is usually touched on day 1** on published picks, so E1's raw rates sit near a ceiling for both arms and the control-adjusted excess compresses toward 0. E1 is kept because H-091 names it and because the *excess* form is the one that can still separate the arms, but §11 decision 4 records the alternative, and the speed companion (§4 secondary 7) is where an L1 effect would show if the rate cannot.
4. **The state is not randomly assigned.** POS_GAMMA concentrates in large, densely-struck, slow names; WALL_IN_PATH additionally depends on how wide the entry-to-target band is in percent, which is `k × atr_pct`. B2 (each arm against its own distance-matched neighbourhood) removes most of this and S2 (§8 clause 6) is the gate for the rest — a raw effect that does not survive S2 is INCONCLUSIVE, not a finding. The `k` and `atr_pct` tercile decompositions are mandatory (§4 secondary 10).
5. **The conjunction may be one of its components.** A D − C gap can come entirely from the regime or entirely from the wall. Both marginal decompositions are registered (§4 secondary 2) so this is always visible, and §9's guide line may not describe a conjunction the decomposition contradicts.
6. **Walls are the top five strikes by |net gamma| — a config default, not a physical set** (`volatilx services/gex.py:30`, `:322-337`), and `side` is the *sign of net gamma at the strike*, not an option type. Both wall readings in §11 decision 1 inherit that, and the alternative reading is always printed.
7. **Strikes are compared to split-adjusted prices.** `gex_context.spot_close` comes from the GEX build and `C_t` from `prices_daily_split`; a split or a stale GEX spot would move every strike relative to the entry. The 2% spot-basis guard (§2.6) removes those rows and its count is reported.
8. **Overlapping 20-session windows and repeated symbols.** Adjacent nights share most of their outcome sessions and the same symbol is re-selected across nights. Three registered protections: the within-night permutation p (exact under label exchangeability, no cross-night independence assumed), the 10-session block bootstrap, and the DP-51 episode-clustered CI, which is a **gate** (§8 clause 3), not a footnote.
9. **The population is a sub-population.** Everything here applies to **GEX-covered published picks** (97.1% of published rows on this window, §2.3) whose lane carries a target (the null-ladder denominator, 1.45% missing on the swing lane). Both arms are covered, so the *contrast* is not biased by coverage; the *generalization* is, and §9's guide line must say so.
10. **The LLM flags are not the state.** `risk_flags` is free text and empty on synthesis fallback (`volatilx ai_agents/super_agent_select_agent.py:440`), so absence of a flag is confounded with synthesis failure.
