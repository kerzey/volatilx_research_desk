## 1. Hypothesis (plain English, one sentence)

**Published picks whose Conviction Monitor tier is still EXIT at the end of its five-day watch go on to a worse path over the next ten sessions than picks the monitor never rated EXIT, with picks whose EXIT softened before day five sitting in between — and the ordering survives matching every pick against same-night candidates that had already fallen just as far.**

This is **H-097** as filed (BACKLOG F6, from the 2026-09-15 census). It is a question about **calibration**: not whether acting on an EXIT pays, but whether the three states the monitor can leave a pick in at the end of its watch **order the path at all**. If they do not, the panel is printing a ranking that means nothing, and that is true whatever Q019 finds.

**The second half of the sentence is the question.** The tier is built from structure breaks, key-level and Fibonacci breaches and bias flips measured against the pick-night snapshot (volatilx `services/conviction_monitor_service.py:328-735`), every one of which is a function of the price path since the pick night; the census found the EXIT tier is in practice "price broke the pick-night support on the 1h and 4h charts" (`research/reports/monitor_counts_2026-09-15/REPORT.md` §3). The grouping variable is therefore **part of the price path**, so **DP-12 governs**: each group is netted against controls **matched on the damage already done by the close of session t+5**, and the unmatched total ordering is **descriptive and licenses no rule**. Without that, a monotone ordering would say no more than "the stocks that had fallen the most kept falling", which is a momentum fact available without the monitor. DP-12's cost travels with the rule: picks with no caliper-matched control are dropped and counted, and the floor arrives later.

**How this differs from Q019, and why it is not a merge (DP-29).** Q019 (locked 2026-09-13, decision 2027-05-24) asks whether **selling at the next open after the first EXIT flag** beats **holding to session t+10** — an action, on a binary flag, over a window that begins on session 2 or 3. Q038 asks whether the **three terminal states of the same five-day watch** rank the path **from the open of session 6**, which Q019 never measures: Q019's arms are FLAG / NOFLAG-as-of-`s`, it reads no monitor row after the first flag by construction (its ordering rule 3), it has no softening dimension at all, and its verdict can be positive while the display is still uninformative — a two-state contrast cannot show that a three-state display is ordered. The two questions also use different control pools (Q019 matches flagged picks to *published* picks, which cost it 68.7% of its flagged picks; this question matches to *same-night unpublished candidates*, the pool H-097 names), a different entry (Q019 `Open_{s+1}`, here `Open_{t+6}`) and a different measurement stretch. They do overlap on calendar bars and population, and §7 states that overlap and forbids counting them as two confirmations of anything.

**Two endpoints are primary** (§4), both paths from the same basis, both netted against damage-matched controls:

- **(P1, the adverse race)** excess rate of touching −1 ATR **before** +1 ATR over sessions t+6..t+15, EXIT-persisting minus never-EXIT;
- **(P2, the favourable reach)** excess rate of touching +1 ATR at all in the same window, same contrast.

**Both must confirm in the concordant sign — P1 positive and P2 negative — before any rule follows** (§8), and **both carry a monotonicity gate**: the middle group must sit between the other two.

**Claim scope, fixed here and carried by every restatement of the verdict (§8, §9), because this question measures a defective surface exactly as subscribers see it today:**

1. **A two-state monitor.** Since 2026-06-01 the polarity arm is dark — `polarity_unavailable_coverage_low` on **100%** of in-scope rows, `polarity_tier` WATCH on every row, **0** `overall_tier` HOLD rows in 2,921 (**PI-014** and its 2026-09-15 addendum; `STEWARD_Q019_exposure.md` §6, §8). `overall_tier = worst(polarity, technical)` (`:792-805`) therefore cannot print HOLD, so "never worse than WATCH" is the best state a pick can reach and the tested ordering is **EXIT vs WATCH**, not the three-tier ladder the product documents.
2. **A technical-arm ordering.** Every in-window tier is the technical arm alone (284 of 284 primary flags in Q019's window). The verdict says nothing about what the monitor would order if polarity were restored; a **dated** restoration inside the window makes the tier a different feature and fires §5.6's window-start rule.
3. **Fed by truncated flow.** The dead arm's input is `uoa_contract_daily`'s aggressor decomposition, which **PI-020** shows is truncated at one page — zero-trade rows stored for every contract the page did not reach, on 317 of 604 published picks since 2026-06-01. The verdict is about the monitor as built on that input, not about a repaired one.

### 2.3 The three groups (a partition, fixed at the close of session t+5, never revisited)

Let `last_i` be the pick's **latest in-scope monitor row**, and `T_i` that row's **`overall_tier`** (the undecayed tier — DECISIONS row 1; on days 3–5 `age_adjusted_severity` is a one-step softening of `overall_tier`, so with HOLD unreachable (PI-014) the day-5 map EXIT→WATCH, WATCH→HOLD is a **bijection on the realized support** and the two fields induce the *same* terminal partition in this window; the displayed field is registered as a printed secondary and the bijection is re-measured at every freeze). **Decision 1 is settled on `overall_tier`** (DECISIONS row 1) and R1 measured both of its grounds, counts only: under `age_adjusted_severity` the persisting group is **0 of 491** picks — structurally empty, not thin — and the day-5 cross-tab is a **bijection on 417 of 417** guard-passed rows (EXIT→WATCH, WATCH→HOLD, no other cell populated; `STEWARD_Q038_exposure.md` item 2):

- **G_A — NEVER_EXIT:** no in-scope row has `overall_tier = 'EXIT'`. (In this window this means "WATCH throughout"; HOLD is unreachable, §1 restriction 1.)
- **G_B — SOFTENED:** ≥ 1 in-scope row is EXIT **and** `T_i ≠ 'EXIT'`.
- **G_C — PERSISTING:** `T_i = 'EXIT'`.

**Eligibility for classification:** the pick must have **≥ 4 distinct in-scope session rows** and `last_i` dated **≥ t+4**. Fewer, or a last row at t+1..t+3, → **excluded and counted** (the watch is truncated and the terminal state is not measured); the distribution of in-scope row counts is printed. The ≥ t+4 rule rather than "a row dated exactly t+5" is deliberate: across a holiday the job's five weekdays cover only four sessions, and requiring t+5 would drop holiday-adjacent nights systematically. Where `last_i` is dated t+4 the classification is one session older than the basis, which is earlier and therefore knowledge-time legal.

`G_B` is defined by the **terminal state**, not by the census's "ever softened on a later day" (which is not a partition: a pick that softened on day 3 and re-printed EXIT on day 5 is in both). The census's definition is a printed secondary (§4), with the overlap against this partition counted, and the EXIT-day dose cells (secondary 5) carry the within-`G_C` detail. Settled as DECISIONS row 4: **the terminal state decides — a pick whose `last_i` is EXIT is in `G_C` whatever it did on days 1–4** — because the per-night group means require **disjoint** groups and the alternative would double-count a soften-then-re-EXIT pick in both `G_B` and `G_C`, making the monotonicity gate uninterpretable.

### 2.4 Definitions (all prices in the signal-date split basis)

`C_t` = the pick's **actual** regular-session close on pick night `t` from `prices_daily_split`;
`ATR` = ATR14 from `prices_daily_split` bars dated ≤ `t`;
`dir` = +1 bullish / −1 bearish from `dominant_direction`;
**`X` = `C_t`** — the DP-11 entry for a position that is already held, used for `dam5`, for the descriptive plan panel and for matching, applied identically to picks and controls;
**`dam5` = `dir × (Close_{t+5} − C_t) / ATR`** — the damage already done, in ATR, at the moment the watch ends. `Close_{t+5}` exists at 16:00 ET on session t+5, two minutes before the monitor's final row and a session before the basis; it is legal at the classification moment;
**`B` = `Open_{t+6}`** — the regular-session open of the session after the watch ends, the first executable moment after the monitor's last word, and **the basis from which both primaries' levels are set**, for picks and controls alike. No level can be passed at entry (rule 5).

### 2.6 Contributing night (the floor unit, DP-21)

**A pick night `t` carrying ≥ 1 gradeable, control-matched pick in `G_A` *and* ≥ 1 in `G_C`.** P1 and P2 are computed on the identical eligible set, so their contributing-night counts are identical by construction and are printed separately anyway. A matured, non-excluded night carrying only one extreme group is a **non-contributing night, not an exclusion**, and is printed in the funnel with its reason.

**The monotonicity gate (§8 clause 6) has its own count:** a **three-group night** carries ≥ 1 matched pick in each of `G_A`, `G_B`, `G_C`. Three-group nights are a subset of contributing nights; the gate needs **≥ 20** of them or it is **not evaluable**, which blocks CONFIRMED (§8). **Measured at R1: 18 of 53 matured nights (0.3396/session)** — the floor clears within ≈ 6 further eligible sessions on any projection including the CI lower bound, so this gate is **not** a designed-in INCONCLUSIVE (DECISIONS row 7).

**The B3 gate (§8 clause 7, as amended by decision 3) has its own count too:** a **B3-contributing night** carries ≥ 1 same-night, same-direction, ±0.25 ATR `dam5`-matched `G_C`→`G_A` pair (§3 B3). Under the two-way gate the endpoint needs **≥ 20** B3-contributing nights or clause 7 is **not evaluable and the verdict is INCONCLUSIVE**. **Measured at R1: 4 of 53 matured nights (0.0755/session, 95% Wilson CI 0.030–0.179), off 4 of 129 matured gradeable `G_C` picks (3.1%)** — the count on which this question is deferred rather than locked (§5.6).

## 3. Baseline(s) — what this must beat

- **B1 (the baseline the hypothesis names): `G_A`, the same nights' never-EXIT published picks**, same tape, same publication process, same clock, same basis. This is "Y's rate when X did not happen, in the same regime". The adjacent pairs `G_B − G_A` and `G_C − G_B` are the within-question ordering contrasts (§4) and are registered, not discovered.
- **B2 (the DP-12 damage-matched control, and the reason the primary is a difference of *excesses*): same-night unpublished candidates at the same damage.** For each eligible pick `i`, take the **10 nearest same-night unpublished `sas_candidates` rows** (the complement of DP-28's predicate, dark-lane rows included) subject to, as hard constraints:
  (a) **`|dam5_c − dam5_i| ≤ 0.25 ATR`** — the caliper, `dam5_c` computed in **`dir_i`** from the candidate's own `C_t` and `Close_{t+5}` (Q019's DP-12 construction, moved to the candidate pool);
  (b) the candidate's own `dominant_direction = dir_i` (a bullish-rated name is never graded as if short);
  (c) `symbol_c ≠ symbol_i`, and the candidate is not published on night `t`.
  From the survivors take the 10 nearest on standardized **`log C_t` / `atr_pct` / `beta60` / `runup20`** (night-cross-sectional median and MAD, Euclidean, ties by symbol ascending, with replacement across picks). A pick obtaining **fewer than 3** controls is dropped and counted. Each control is graded on **`i`'s own calendar window** — basis `Open_{t+6}`, levels `± 1 ATR` from **its own** basis and **its own** ATR, sessions t+6..t+15 — so both sides carry a level at the identical ATR distance and the arms differ in **whether the monitor said anything about them** and in nothing else. Post-match standardized mean differences on `dam5`, `atr_pct`, `beta60`, `runup20` and `log C_t` are printed per night and per group; **`|SMD| > 0.25` on any of them is declared a large residual imbalance and printed beside the verdict** — a disclosure, not a gate.
  **Why the control pool is unpublished candidates and not published picks (the Q019 pool):** H-097 names it, and Q019 measured what the published pool costs — **195 of 284 flagged picks (68.7%) dropped for want of a caliper-matched published control, the dropped tail systematically the more damaged one** (mean `dam` −0.94 vs −0.17 ATR). A pool of ≈ 42 unpublished candidates a night instead of ≈ 8 published picks is what lets the caliper hold **without** discarding the damaged tail, which is the part of the distribution a calibration question most needs.
- **B3 (the within-published damage pairing — `S2`, a two-way gate, never a primary):** 1:1 within-night matching of `G_C` picks to `G_A` picks on `dam5` (±0.25 ATR caliper), same direction, without replacement, ties by symbol ascending; unmatched picks dropped and counted. This is the "is it the tier, or is it which picks get flagged" read done entirely inside the published slate. **Decision 3 (DEFAULTED under R-5 / DP-45) makes it a two-way gate:** CONFIRMED requires B3 to clear **≥ 20 B3-contributing nights** (§2.6) **and** to be **beyond MPE (5.0 pp) in the same sign as the primary**; **B3 inside MPE, beyond MPE in the opposite sign, or not evaluable (< 20 B3-contributing nights) makes the endpoint INCONCLUSIVE** (§8 clause 7). B3 can still **never create** a CONFIRMED, it takes **no BH slot** (m stays 2, §7), and it prints raw p only. **It is a thin statistic and its failure is a likely route to INCONCLUSIVE** (§5.4): Q019 measured a 68.7% drop rate for exactly this published-to-published pairing, and R1 measures **4 of 129** matured `G_C` picks obtaining a partner here (3.1%), which is why this question is deferred rather than locked (§5.6). **The one-way reading — B3 blocks only on a contradicting sign and never on thinness — is the alternative that was not taken**, and it is the text this file reverts to under re-entry route 2 (header, §5.6).
- **B4 (the total ordering; descriptive, licenses no rule — DP-12):** the same three-group contrasts on **raw** rates with no control and no `dam5` matching, printed beside every primary and labelled *"total effect: includes the fall that produced the tier; descriptive, does not decide"*.

## 4. Objective metric (rule 5 — the price path from a stated basis)

**Basis.** `B = Open_{t+6}` for picks and controls alike (§2.4). Levels are set **from `B`**: favourable `B + dir × 1.0 × ATR`, adverse `B − dir × 1.0 × ATR`. A level is never passed at entry by construction. **No stop is assumed and no exit is taken** (rule 5, DP-02); the adverse level is a measurement line, not an order.

**The two events, per row, over sessions `t+6 … t+15` (10 sessions, H-097 as filed — DP-25):**

- **ADV = 1** iff the adverse level is touched **before** the favourable one (neither touched → 0);
- **FAV = 1** iff the favourable level is touched at all in the window.

**Ordering rules, applied by `eval.py` with no judgment:**
1. Same-session adverse and favourable touches are ordered from `prices_hourly_raw`, converted to the signal-date split basis (`eval.py` asserts basis agreement per symbol-date and fails loudly). Where one hourly bar holds both, **or the symbol has no hourly bars**, the pair counts **adverse-first — DP-27 applied literally and symmetrically to both arms and all three groups**. The count resolved by this rule is printed **per group and per arm**. This matters here in a way it did not in Q019: `manifest_prices_v001` carries hourly bars for **published symbols only** (227 of 436 symbols), so on the sealed portion a large share of unpublished-control same-session ties resolves by rule — R1 measured the **row-weighted** coverage of the B2 pool at **72.3%** (2,116 of 2,927 valid pool rows; 215 of 391 distinct unpublished-candidate symbols), i.e. the rule binds on ≈ **27.7%** of control-side ties rather than the ≈ 48% a symbol-count reading suggests (`STEWARD_Q038_exposure.md` item 8). The bias is common to all three groups and cancels to first order in a difference of excesses; **R2 requests hourly bars for every in-window candidate symbol, published and unpublished, on the successor freezes**, and `eval.py` prints the sealed-vs-successor tie rates separately.
   **The opposite reading is a one-way blocker, not a printed sensitivity (Correction 3, DP-27).** Because on the sealed portion the convention decides a real share of the control-side ties, the favourable-first reading is computed in the same `eval.py` pass and: **if it flips the sign of a primary, that endpoint is INCONCLUSIVE** (§8). It can **never** create a CONFIRMED, it takes **no BH slot**, and it prints raw p only.
2. A gap through a level at an open is a touch (DP-26), including at the `t+6` open itself.
3. Touching a level retires nothing and ends nothing.

**Excess, per pick:** `exc_i = EVENT_i − mean(EVENT over i's B2 controls)`, computed separately for ADV and FAV.

**Primary endpoints (two, pre-specified, BH-corrected within the question and across F6 — §7).** For pick night `t`, `mean_G[·]` is the mean over that night's eligible picks in group `G`:

- **P1 — the adverse race.** `Δ1_t = mean_{G_C}[exc^{ADV}] − mean_{G_A}[exc^{ADV}]`; estimand **`m1 = mean_t Δ1_t`** over contributing nights, in **percentage points**.
- **P2 — the favourable reach.** `Δ2_t = mean_{G_C}[exc^{FAV}] − mean_{G_A}[exc^{FAV}]`; estimand **`m2 = mean_t Δ2_t`**, same units.

Both are **two-sided**. H-097 predicts a calibrated monitor: **`m1 > 0` and `m2 < 0`** (the persisting-EXIT group races adverse more often and reaches the favourable level less often). A result beyond MPE in the reversed sign is a confirmed finding of the same weight — the display would be ordering the path **backwards** — and §9 writes both branches.

**The filed "ATR move" is registered as descriptive, and this is a re-specification the desk records rather than applies silently.** H-097 names "the adverse-first rate and the ATR move" as its two endpoints. `dir × (Close_{t+15} − Open_{t+6}) / ATR` is a **fixed-horizon close-to-close return**, which **CLAUDE.md rule 5 and DP-01** make secondary and descriptive; it never decides here. Rule 5 is a non-negotiable and outranks DP-25's "take the hypothesis as filed", which is a Default. The quantity is computed, printed per group with its own CI and a 0.25 ATR reference line (DP-10), and labelled *"fixed-horizon return: descriptive, does not decide"*. **P2 is its path-legal counterpart** — the unconditional touch-within-k form, which DP-44 states is the form that decides — so nothing the filed hypothesis asked about is dropped.

**Registered secondaries — printed always, and never a result (each prints raw p only, marked "descriptive, does not decide"; none takes a BH slot).** Registered here so none of them can be searched for at the decision pass. **Three of them can block a CONFIRMED and none can create one:** the adjacent contrasts through §8 clause 6, **B3 through the two-way clause 7** (decision 3), and the favourable-first tie reading through clause 9.

### 5.4 Precision and power at the MPE (DP-57 — the floor does not guarantee precision)

Each night's `Δ_t` is a difference of two thin-group means of a bounded excess. Taking the per-pick excess SD at ≈ 0.45 and groups of 1–4 picks a night, the per-night SD is ≈ 0.40–0.45 in rate units, so at **N = 80** contributing nights `SE(m) ≈ 4.7–5.0 pp` and at **N = 115** `SE(m) ≈ 4.0–4.2 pp`.

- **Minimum detectable effect at 80% power, two-sided α = 0.048: ≈ 13–14 pp at N = 80, ≈ 11–12 pp at N = 115.** The design is powered for a **large** ordering effect, not for one at the MPE. A monitor that orders the path by 5–12 pp will most often return INCONCLUSIVE, and that is registered here rather than discovered afterwards.
- **NULL as written in §8 is an equivalence claim** and needs a CI half-width ≤ 5 pp, i.e. ≈ 320 contributing nights — beyond DP-43's 12-month ceiling on this slate. **NULL is therefore unlikely to be reachable from this run**, and §8 says so; the floors are not lowered to manufacture it. The practical NULL-ish outcome is a tight INCONCLUSIVE, which §9 treats as evidence for neither keeping nor removing the surface.
- **The clause-7 gate is a thin statistic, and its failure is a likely route to INCONCLUSIVE** (decision 3, §3 B3, §8 clause 7). B3 pairs `G_C` to `G_A` inside one night on a ±0.25 ATR `dam5` caliper, and the two groups are **nearly disjoint in that very covariate** — R1 measures retained `dam5` means of **+0.47** (`G_A`) against **−1.27** (`G_C`), dropped tails +1.65 against −2.26 — so the pairing survives on **3.1%** of `G_C` picks (4 of 129) and 20 B3-contributing nights is a floor no window inside the ceiling reaches at the pessimistic rate. Under a two-way gate a thin B3 forces INCONCLUSIVE **whatever the registered contrast does**, which is the reason this question is deferred rather than locked (§5.6) and not a reason to weaken the gate (DECISIONS row 11).
- **Dependence:** adjacent nights' 10-session windows overlap by up to 90% and the same symbol is re-selected across nights. The block bootstrap and the DP-51 episode bootstrap price both; the within-night permutation p is immune to the first.
- **Multiplicity:** two positively-dependent primaries within the question (ADV and FAV are the same race read two ways) and 17 primaries in F6 (§7). BH is conservative under positive dependence. **The DP-58 interim is withdrawn** (§5.5, DECISIONS row 10), so its α is unspent and the fixed look decides at **α = 0.05** unless a re-entry reinstates the interim, in which case the spend and the 0.048 fixed look return with it.
- **Strata:** a sub-cell needs 20 contributing nights, so at the central rate a cell clears by holding ≈ 17% of contributing nights; bearish (2.5% of rows) and `< 80` (1 row in 579) cannot and are SUPPRESSED at lock (§4).
