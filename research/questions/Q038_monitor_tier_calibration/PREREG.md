## 1. Hypothesis (plain English, one sentence)

**Published picks whose Conviction Monitor tier is still EXIT at the end of its five-day watch go on to a worse path over the next ten sessions than picks the monitor never rated EXIT, with picks whose EXIT softened before day five sitting in between — and the ordering survives matching every pick against same-night candidates that had already fallen just as far.**

This is **H-097** as filed (BACKLOG F6, from the 2026-09-15 census). It is a question about **calibration**: not whether acting on an EXIT pays, but whether the three states the monitor can leave a pick in at the end of its watch **order the path at all**. If they do not, the panel is printing a ranking that means nothing, and that is true whatever Q019 finds.

**The second half of the sentence is the question.** The tier is built from structure breaks, key-level and Fibonacci breaches and bias flips measured against the pick-night snapshot (volatilx `services/conviction_monitor_service.py:328-735`), every one of which is a function of the price path since the pick night; the census found the EXIT tier is in practice "price broke the pick-night support on the 1h and 4h charts" (`research/reports/monitor_counts_2026-09-15/REPORT.md` §3). The grouping variable is therefore **part of the price path**, so **DP-12 governs**: each group is netted against controls **matched on the damage already done by the close of session t+5**, and the unmatched total ordering is **descriptive and licenses no rule**. Without that, a monotone ordering would say no more than "the stocks that had fallen the most kept falling", which is a momentum fact available without the monitor. DP-12's cost travels with the rule: picks with no caliper-matched control are dropped and counted, and the floor arrives later.

**How this differs from Q019, and why it is not a merge (DP-29).** Q019 (locked 2026-09-13, decision 2027-05-24) asks whether **selling at the next open after the first EXIT flag** beats **holding to session t+10** — an action, on a binary flag, over a window that begins on session 2 or 3. Q038 asks whether the **three terminal states of the same five-day watch** rank the path **from the open of session 6**, which Q019 never measures: Q019's arms are FLAG / NOFLAG-as-of-`s`, it reads no monitor row after the first flag by construction (its ordering rule 3), it has no softening dimension at all, and its verdict can be positive while the display is still uninformative — a two-state contrast cannot show that a three-state display is ordered. The two questions also use different control pools (Q019 matches flagged picks to *published* picks, which cost it 68.7% of its flagged picks; this question matches to *same-night unpublished candidates*, the pool H-097 names), a different entry (Q019 `Open_{s+1}`, here `Open_{t+6}`) and a different measurement stretch. They do overlap on calendar bars and population, and §7 states that overlap and forbids counting them as two confirmations of anything.

**Two endpoints are primary** (§4), both paths from the same basis, both netted against damage-matched controls:

- **(P1, the adverse race)** excess rate of touching −1 ATR **before** +1 ATR over sessions t+6..t+15, EXIT-persisting minus never-EXIT;
- **(P2, the favourable reach)** excess rate of touching +1 ATR at all in the same window, same contrast.

**Both must confirm in the concordant sign — P1 positive and P2 negative — before any rule follows** (§8), and **both carry a monotonicity gate**: the middle group must sit between the other two.

**Claim scope, fixed here and carried by every restatement of the verdict (§8, §9), because this question measures a defective surface exactly as subscribers see it today:**

1. **A two-state monitor.** Since 2026-06-01 the polarity arm is dark — `polarity_unavailable_coverage_low` on **100%** of in-scope rows, `polarity_tier` WATCH on every row, **0** `overall_tier` HOLD rows in 2,921 (**PI-014** and its 2026-09-15 addendum; `STEWARD_Q019_exposure.md` §6, §8). `overall_tier = worst(polarity, technical)` (`:792-805`) therefore cannot print HOLD, so "never worse than WATCH" is the best state a pick can reach and the tested ordering is **EXIT vs WATCH**, not the three-tier ladder the product documents.
2. **A technical-arm ordering.** Every in-window tier is the technical arm alone (284 of 284 primary flags in Q019's window). The verdict says nothing about what the monitor would order if polarity were restored; a **dated** restoration inside the window makes the tier a different feature and fires §5.6's window-start rule.
3. **Fed by truncated flow.** The dead arm's input is `uoa_contract_daily`'s aggressor decomposition, which **PI-020** shows is truncated at one page — zero-trade rows stored for every contract the page did not reach, on 317 of 604 published picks since 2026-06-01. The verdict is about the monitor as built on that input, not about a repaired one.

## 2. Population

### 2.1 Unit and sources

- **Unit of inference: the trading night** (rule 6), and the night is the **pick night `t`**. Pick rows are aggregated within a night before any inference; control rows never add to n.
- **Selections / monitor (`manifest_v001` + successors):** `sas_candidates` (`qualified`, `selected_rank`, `overall_score`, `dominant_direction`, `best_timeframe`, `trading_date`, `symbol`); `sas_runs.finished_at` (DP-04); `market_regime_daily` (stratum only, nights ≥ 2026-06-09); `conviction_monitor` (`trading_date`, `original_pick_date`, `symbol`, `days_since_qualified`, `overall_tier`, `age_adjusted_severity`, `polarity_tier`, `technical_overall_tier`, `reason_codes_json`, `created_at`, `updated_at`).
- **Prices (`manifest_prices_v001` / `manifest_prices_universe_v001` + successors):** `prices_daily_split` (`C_t`, `Close_{t+5}`, `Open_{t+6}`, forward bars, ATR14, `beta60`, `runup20`, SPY tape), `prices_daily_raw` (split-basis assertions only), `prices_hourly_raw` (same-session ordering of the ±1 ATR race).
- **Never an input:** `sas_selection_excursion`, `outcome_*`, `level_hit_*`, `outcome_target_invalid` (calendar windows, close basis, recomputed weeks later — `volatilx services/sas_excursion.py:8-18`, `:336-354`); `uoa_symbol_daily.fwd_return_*` (banned, FREEZE_v001 §5); the platform's `atr_pct` and `spot_close` (corrupted around splits / stale on re-run nights — DATA_NOTES, PI-003, EXPLORE_001 §9).

### 2.2 Picks in scope

Every **published** pick — `qualified IS TRUE AND selected_rank IS NOT NULL` (**DP-28**; dark-lane rows are in no group and remain available to the control pool) — on a non-excluded pick night `t` in the §6 window. **Not elite-only:** the monitor job loads every qualified pick with no score filter (`services/conviction_monitor_service.py:1043-1051`); the Elite gate is on the *surface* (`routers/conviction_monitor.py:32`), not on the rows. Score bands are sub-cells, never groups.

**Monitor rows in scope, per pick:** rows with `original_pick_date = t`, matching `symbol`, and `trading_date = s` for a **session `s ∈ t+1 … t+5`**. The row's own `trading_date` is authoritative throughout; `days_since_qualified` is used only for sub-cells (the job's window is five *business* days, not five sessions — Q019 threat 6).

### 2.3 The three groups (a partition, fixed at the close of session t+5, never revisited)

Let `last_i` be the pick's **latest in-scope monitor row**, and `T_i` that row's **`overall_tier`** (the undecayed tier — §11 decision 1; on days 3–5 `age_adjusted_severity` is a one-step softening of `overall_tier`, so with HOLD unreachable (PI-014) the day-5 map EXIT→WATCH, WATCH→HOLD is a **bijection on the realized support** and the two fields induce the *same* terminal partition in this window; the displayed field is registered as a printed secondary and the bijection is re-measured at every freeze):

- **G_A — NEVER_EXIT:** no in-scope row has `overall_tier = 'EXIT'`. (In this window this means "WATCH throughout"; HOLD is unreachable, §1 restriction 1.)
- **G_B — SOFTENED:** ≥ 1 in-scope row is EXIT **and** `T_i ≠ 'EXIT'`.
- **G_C — PERSISTING:** `T_i = 'EXIT'`.

**Eligibility for classification:** the pick must have **≥ 4 distinct in-scope session rows** and `last_i` dated **≥ t+4**. Fewer, or a last row at t+1..t+3, → **excluded and counted** (the watch is truncated and the terminal state is not measured); the distribution of in-scope row counts is printed. The ≥ t+4 rule rather than "a row dated exactly t+5" is deliberate: across a holiday the job's five weekdays cover only four sessions, and requiring t+5 would drop holiday-adjacent nights systematically. Where `last_i` is dated t+4 the classification is one session older than the basis, which is earlier and therefore knowledge-time legal.

`G_B` is defined by the **terminal state**, not by the census's "ever softened on a later day" (which is not a partition: a pick that softened on day 3 and re-printed EXIT on day 5 is in both). The census's definition is a printed secondary (§4). See §11 decision 4.

### 2.4 Definitions (all prices in the signal-date split basis)

`C_t` = the pick's **actual** regular-session close on pick night `t` from `prices_daily_split`;
`ATR` = ATR14 from `prices_daily_split` bars dated ≤ `t`;
`dir` = +1 bullish / −1 bearish from `dominant_direction`;
**`X` = `C_t`** — the DP-11 entry for a position that is already held, used for `dam5`, for the descriptive plan panel and for matching, applied identically to picks and controls;
**`dam5` = `dir × (Close_{t+5} − C_t) / ATR`** — the damage already done, in ATR, at the moment the watch ends. `Close_{t+5}` exists at 16:00 ET on session t+5, two minutes before the monitor's final row and a session before the basis; it is legal at the classification moment;
**`B` = `Open_{t+6}`** — the regular-session open of the session after the watch ends, the first executable moment after the monitor's last word, and **the basis from which both primaries' levels are set**, for picks and controls alike. No level can be passed at entry (rule 5).

### 2.5 Exclusions — each counted and printed in the results header, never silently dropped

Every exclusion uses only inputs available at the decision it governs, the trading calendar, or a measurement failure.

- pick nights in **`exclusions_v003.json`** ∪ the add-only successor files. In-window on the sealed portion: **2026-06-26, 2026-07-02, 2026-07-06**.
- any night whose `finished_at` postdates the next session's open (**DP-04**).
- **monitor rows that are not point-in-time** — dropped row by row and counted: `created_at` not on the row's own `trading_date` before the next session's open, or `updated_at` postdating `created_at` by more than one minute. The monitor deletes and reinserts a date's rows (`:1097-1101`) and its entry point accepts an arbitrary `--trading-date` (`scripts/run_conviction_monitor.py:38-62`), so the guard is what makes the declared 16:02 ET availability checkable per row. A pick that loses a row to the guard is re-tested against §2.3's ≥ 4-row rule and, if it fails, is **excluded and counted — never reassigned to another group**. Measured in-window: **0 failures** June–September; all 160 in the freeze are May 2026 (`STEWARD_Q019_exposure.md` §5).
- published picks with **fewer than 4 in-scope monitor rows** or `last_i` before t+4 → excluded and counted. (Measured precedent: **0 of 465** published picks on Q019's 58 matured nights had *zero* in-scope rows; the ≥ 4-row rate is R1.)
- picks with no `C_t`, no `Close_{t+5}`, no `Open_{t+6}`, or fewer than 60 daily bars dated ≤ `t` → excluded and counted (measured 0 / 0 on Q019's funnel for the first and last).
- **ungradeable** picks: gradeable iff every session t+6..t+15 has a daily bar, or the race is already determined by the bars that exist. Anything else (halt, delisting, acquisition) → excluded and counted. **This can never move a pick between groups** — groups are fixed before any bar dated after session t+5 is read. A night is dropped if > 25% of its eligible picks are ungradeable.
- **picks with no valid damage-matched control** (§3 B2) → excluded and counted, with the `dam5` distribution of the dropped and the retained printed **by month and by half**. This is DP-12's cost, paid and printed; **the caliper is not widened to retain the tail**.
- pick nights whose session **t+15** falls after the last trading date of the pinned price freeze → **immature**, excluded and counted. Maturity comes from the trading calendar; right-censoring is never graded as a non-touch.

### 2.6 Contributing night (the floor unit, DP-21)

**A pick night `t` carrying ≥ 1 gradeable, control-matched pick in `G_A` *and* ≥ 1 in `G_C`.** P1 and P2 are computed on the identical eligible set, so their contributing-night counts are identical by construction and are printed separately anyway. A matured, non-excluded night carrying only one extreme group is a **non-contributing night, not an exclusion**, and is printed in the funnel with its reason.

**The monotonicity gate (§8 clause 6) has its own count:** a **three-group night** carries ≥ 1 matched pick in each of `G_A`, `G_B`, `G_C`. Three-group nights are a subset of contributing nights; the gate needs **≥ 20** of them or it is **not evaluable**, which blocks CONFIRMED (§8).

## 3. Baseline(s) — what this must beat

- **B1 (the baseline the hypothesis names): `G_A`, the same nights' never-EXIT published picks**, same tape, same publication process, same clock, same basis. This is "Y's rate when X did not happen, in the same regime". The adjacent pairs `G_B − G_A` and `G_C − G_B` are the within-question ordering contrasts (§4) and are registered, not discovered.
- **B2 (the DP-12 damage-matched control, and the reason the primary is a difference of *excesses*): same-night unpublished candidates at the same damage.** For each eligible pick `i`, take the **10 nearest same-night unpublished `sas_candidates` rows** (the complement of DP-28's predicate, dark-lane rows included) subject to, as hard constraints:
  (a) **`|dam5_c − dam5_i| ≤ 0.25 ATR`** — the caliper, `dam5_c` computed in **`dir_i`** from the candidate's own `C_t` and `Close_{t+5}` (Q019's DP-12 construction, moved to the candidate pool);
  (b) the candidate's own `dominant_direction = dir_i` (a bullish-rated name is never graded as if short);
  (c) `symbol_c ≠ symbol_i`, and the candidate is not published on night `t`.
  From the survivors take the 10 nearest on standardized **`log C_t` / `atr_pct` / `beta60` / `runup20`** (night-cross-sectional median and MAD, Euclidean, ties by symbol ascending, with replacement across picks). A pick obtaining **fewer than 3** controls is dropped and counted. Each control is graded on **`i`'s own calendar window** — basis `Open_{t+6}`, levels `± 1 ATR` from **its own** basis and **its own** ATR, sessions t+6..t+15 — so both sides carry a level at the identical ATR distance and the arms differ in **whether the monitor said anything about them** and in nothing else. Post-match standardized mean differences on `dam5`, `atr_pct`, `beta60`, `runup20` and `log C_t` are printed per night and per group; **`|SMD| > 0.25` on any of them is declared a large residual imbalance and printed beside the verdict** — a disclosure, not a gate.
  **Why the control pool is unpublished candidates and not published picks (the Q019 pool):** H-097 names it, and Q019 measured what the published pool costs — **195 of 284 flagged picks (68.7%) dropped for want of a caliper-matched published control, the dropped tail systematically the more damaged one** (mean `dam` −0.94 vs −0.17 ATR). A pool of ≈ 42 unpublished candidates a night instead of ≈ 8 published picks is what lets the caliper hold **without** discarding the damaged tail, which is the part of the distribution a calibration question most needs.
- **B3 (the within-published damage pairing — `S2`, a one-way blocker, never a primary):** 1:1 within-night matching of `G_C` picks to `G_A` picks on `dam5` (±0.25 ATR caliper), same direction, without replacement, ties by symbol ascending; unmatched picks dropped and counted. This is the "is it the tier, or is it which picks get flagged" read done entirely inside the published slate. On Q019's measured drop rate it may well be thin or unevaluable, which is why §8 clause 7 makes it **one-way**: if it clears 20 contributing nights and is beyond MPE in the **opposite** sign it blocks CONFIRMED, and it can **never** create one. See §11 decision 3.
- **B4 (the total ordering; descriptive, licenses no rule — DP-12):** the same three-group contrasts on **raw** rates with no control and no `dam5` matching, printed beside every primary and labelled *"total effect: includes the fall that produced the tier; descriptive, does not decide"*.

## 4. Objective metric (rule 5 — the price path from a stated basis)

**Basis.** `B = Open_{t+6}` for picks and controls alike (§2.4). Levels are set **from `B`**: favourable `B + dir × 1.0 × ATR`, adverse `B − dir × 1.0 × ATR`. A level is never passed at entry by construction. **No stop is assumed and no exit is taken** (rule 5, DP-02); the adverse level is a measurement line, not an order.

**The two events, per row, over sessions `t+6 … t+15` (10 sessions, H-097 as filed — DP-25):**

- **ADV = 1** iff the adverse level is touched **before** the favourable one (neither touched → 0);
- **FAV = 1** iff the favourable level is touched at all in the window.

**Ordering rules, applied by `eval.py` with no judgment:**
1. Same-session adverse and favourable touches are ordered from `prices_hourly_raw`, converted to the signal-date split basis (`eval.py` asserts basis agreement per symbol-date and fails loudly). Where one hourly bar holds both, **or the symbol has no hourly bars**, the pair counts **adverse-first — DP-27 applied literally and symmetrically to both arms and all three groups**. The count resolved by this rule is printed **per group and per arm**, and the opposite reading is a named sensitivity that never decides. This matters here in a way it did not in Q019: `manifest_prices_v001` carries hourly bars for **published symbols only** (227 of 436), so on the sealed portion every unpublished control's same-session tie resolves by rule. The bias is common to all three groups and cancels to first order in a difference of excesses; **R2 requests hourly bars for every in-window candidate symbol, published and unpublished, on the successor freezes**, and `eval.py` prints the sealed-vs-successor tie rates separately.
2. A gap through a level at an open is a touch (DP-26), including at the `t+6` open itself.
3. Touching a level retires nothing and ends nothing.

**Excess, per pick:** `exc_i = EVENT_i − mean(EVENT over i's B2 controls)`, computed separately for ADV and FAV.

**Primary endpoints (two, pre-specified, BH-corrected within the question and across F6 — §7).** For pick night `t`, `mean_G[·]` is the mean over that night's eligible picks in group `G`:

- **P1 — the adverse race.** `Δ1_t = mean_{G_C}[exc^{ADV}] − mean_{G_A}[exc^{ADV}]`; estimand **`m1 = mean_t Δ1_t`** over contributing nights, in **percentage points**.
- **P2 — the favourable reach.** `Δ2_t = mean_{G_C}[exc^{FAV}] − mean_{G_A}[exc^{FAV}]`; estimand **`m2 = mean_t Δ2_t`**, same units.

Both are **two-sided**. H-097 predicts a calibrated monitor: **`m1 > 0` and `m2 < 0`** (the persisting-EXIT group races adverse more often and reaches the favourable level less often). A result beyond MPE in the reversed sign is a confirmed finding of the same weight — the display would be ordering the path **backwards** — and §9 writes both branches.

**The filed "ATR move" is registered as descriptive, and this is a re-specification the desk records rather than applies silently.** H-097 names "the adverse-first rate and the ATR move" as its two endpoints. `dir × (Close_{t+15} − Open_{t+6}) / ATR` is a **fixed-horizon close-to-close return**, which **CLAUDE.md rule 5 and DP-01** make secondary and descriptive; it never decides here. Rule 5 is a non-negotiable and outranks DP-25's "take the hypothesis as filed", which is a Default. The quantity is computed, printed per group with its own CI and a 0.25 ATR reference line (DP-10), and labelled *"fixed-horizon return: descriptive, does not decide"*. **P2 is its path-legal counterpart** — the unconditional touch-within-k form, which DP-44 states is the form that decides — so nothing the filed hypothesis asked about is dropped.

**Registered secondaries — printed always, deciding never (each prints raw p only, marked "descriptive, does not decide").** Registered here so none of them can be searched for at the decision pass.

1. **The two adjacent contrasts** `G_B − G_A` and `G_C − G_B` on both endpoints, with their own CIs — the material for §8's monotonicity gate, printed whether the gate passes or not.
2. **B3 (`S2`)** and **B4** (§3).
3. **The displayed-field partition:** the same three groups built from `age_adjusted_severity` instead of `overall_tier`, with the day-5 cross-tab of the two fields printed, so §2.3's bijection claim is **measured** at every freeze and not assumed.
4. **The census partition:** `G_B` defined as "EXIT on some day and non-EXIT on some later day" (re-EXITs included), with the overlap against §2.3's partition printed.
5. **Dose within `G_C`:** number of EXIT days in the watch (1..5), first-EXIT day (1/2 vs 3–5), and whether the pick was EXIT on **both** days 4 and 5.
6. **The technical-arm decomposition** — per-timeframe tiers (30m/1h/4h/1d/1wk) on the terminal row, and the reason-code composition of `G_C` vs `G_B`. **Recorded now as structurally undecomposable on the polarity side** (0 polarity EXIT rows since 2026-06-01, PI-014): the polarity firing arm is **not measurable in this window, with the reason**, and is never restated as "the technical arm carries all the information", which would be a claim about the tier logic where the fact is about data availability.
7. **Speed (DP-08):** sessions from `B` to the adverse and to the favourable touch, per group, with medians and survival curves. Sessions-to-touch differences are **descriptive and carry no MPE** (DP-44).
8. **Excursion:** maximum adverse and maximum favourable excursion in ATR from `B` over t+6..t+15, per group and per arm; counter-direction touches (reported, never an exit — DP-02).
9. **Shape sensitivities:** the race window at 5 and 20 sessions instead of 10; levels at ±0.5 and ±1.5 ATR; the basis at the **close of session t+5** (picks only, explicitly *not executable*).
10. **The already-held plan panel:** each group's realized ATR per trade from `X = C_t` held to the close of t+15, so the excess rates are never read as money on their own (rule 5).
11. **Sub-cells at ≥ 20 contributing nights:** score band (`< 80` / 80–85 / 85–90 / 90+), direction, **`dam5` tercile**, `atr_pct` tercile, `best_timeframe` lane, tape stratum, regime label, and the in-scope-row-count cell. Below 20 → **SUPPRESSED**, counts only, never "small n, directionally".

**SUPPRESSED at lock — decided now, on measured splits, and never revisited once counts are visible (DP-43).** **(a) direction = bearish** — 14 of 550 published rows on this window (2.5%, `STEWARD_Q017_exposure.md` item 1); **(b) score band `< 80`** — 1 published row in 579 (`STEWARD_Q035_exposure.md`); **(c) the polarity firing arm** — 0 rows, structural, never cured by waiting (PI-014). **(d) score band 90+** is suppressed at lock **unless R1's measured count projects ≥ 20 contributing nights at the decision date**, with the projection printed either way (Q019 measured 2 of 42 matched-contributing nights at 90+, and PI-010's thinning elite count makes even that optimistic). **Suppression restricts affirmative reporting only:** a suppressed cell keeps its printed counts, is never removed from §8 clauses 5, 6 or 7, never by itself makes an endpoint INCONCLUSIVE, and is never a route to an easier CONFIRMED.

**Quotability:** every window here is ≤ 20 sessions → **every number in this question is NON_QUOTABLE** (rule 12).

**Inference.** The unit is the trading night (rule 6); control rows never add to inferential n.

- **p-value (decides significance):** a **within-night permutation of the group label** across the night's eligible picks, preserving each night's group counts, **10,000 permutations, seed 20260915**, per endpoint. Exact under "the terminal tier is exchangeable within the night given the matched controls", and free of the cross-night dependence that overlapping 10-session windows create.
- **CI (decides the excludes-zero clause):** a **stationary block bootstrap** over the ordered contributing nights, **expected block length 8 sessions** (half the 15-session maturity leg; fixed here, not chosen after seeing results), 2,000 resamples, seed 20260915. The plain date-clustered CI is printed beside it.
- **Episode-clustered CI (DP-51, mandatory):** an **episode** is one symbol's run of selections with gaps of ≤ 10 sessions between successive selections (the TI-003 / Q003 window); the bootstrap resamples **episodes and nights jointly**, 2,000 resamples, same seed. A primary clearing the MPE on the block CI and not on the episode CI is **INCONCLUSIVE, never CONFIRMED** (§8).
- **Every estimate prints:** n(contributing nights), n(three-group nights), n(contributing nights dated after the lock commit), n(picks per group), n(controls and control-set sizes), n(picks dropped for < 3 controls), n(dropped for the `dam5` caliper with their `dam5` distribution), n(resolved by ordering rule 1, per group and per arm), n(excluded, by reason), and the post-match SMDs.

### 5.4 Precision and power at the MPE (DP-57 — the floor does not guarantee precision)

Each night's `Δ_t` is a difference of two thin-group means of a bounded excess. Taking the per-pick excess SD at ≈ 0.45 and groups of 1–4 picks a night, the per-night SD is ≈ 0.40–0.45 in rate units, so at **N = 80** contributing nights `SE(m) ≈ 4.7–5.0 pp` and at **N = 115** `SE(m) ≈ 4.0–4.2 pp`.

- **Minimum detectable effect at 80% power, two-sided α = 0.048: ≈ 13–14 pp at N = 80, ≈ 11–12 pp at N = 115.** The design is powered for a **large** ordering effect, not for one at the MPE. A monitor that orders the path by 5–12 pp will most often return INCONCLUSIVE, and that is registered here rather than discovered afterwards.
- **NULL as written in §8 is an equivalence claim** and needs a CI half-width ≤ 5 pp, i.e. ≈ 320 contributing nights — beyond DP-43's 12-month ceiling on this slate. **NULL is therefore unlikely to be reachable from this run**, and §8 says so; the floors are not lowered to manufacture it. The practical NULL-ish outcome is a tight INCONCLUSIVE, which §9 treats as evidence for neither keeping nor removing the surface.
- **Dependence:** adjacent nights' 10-session windows overlap by up to 90% and the same symbol is re-selected across nights. The block bootstrap and the DP-51 episode bootstrap price both; the within-night permutation p is immune to the first.
- **Multiplicity:** two positively-dependent primaries within the question (ADV and FAV are the same race read two ways) and 17 primaries in F6 (§7). BH is conservative under positive dependence; the DP-58 interim spends α (§5.5) and the fixed look decides at **0.048**.
- **Strata:** a sub-cell needs 20 contributing nights, so at the central rate a cell clears by holding ≈ 17% of contributing nights; bearish (2.5% of rows) and `< 80` (1 row in 579) cannot and are SUPPRESSED at lock (§4).

## 8. Decision rule (numeric, written before unsealing)

`m1` and `m2` are in **percentage points of control-adjusted excess rate**, `G_C` minus `G_A`, averaged over contributing nights. Both two-sided; each verdict carries its sign. H-097 predicts **`m1 > 0`** (persisting EXIT races adverse more often) and **`m2 < 0`** (and reaches the favourable level less often).

**MPE: 5.0 pp on each primary** (**DP-20**, **DP-44** — the standing number for a control-adjusted touch-rate endpoint; the Q037 precedent for a difference of two control-netted arm means, where no uplift was taken either). No uplift is proposed. **"Real but below MPE" is INCONCLUSIVE** (rule 6), and §5.4 records that an effect near the MPE will usually land there.

**Claim scope, carried by every restatement of whatever verdict comes out** (§1, §9): a **two-state** monitor (HOLD unreachable, PI-014), a **technical-arm** tier, fed by **truncated** flow on the dead arm (PI-020), over **five days** of watch and **ten** sessions of path. These are scope, not gates; no threshold below moves because of them.

Per endpoint:

- **HISTORICALLY_CONFIRMED** requires **all** of:
  1. **≥ 80 contributing nights** and **≥ 30 dated after the lock commit** (DP-21, DP-24), **≥ 20 three-group nights**, and **≥ 20 contributing nights for any sub-cell that is reported** — a sub-cell below floor is **SUPPRESSED** (counts only) and does not by itself make the endpoint INCONCLUSIVE; the cells suppressed **at lock** (§4) stay suppressed whatever they measure, and their suppression restricts affirmative reporting only;
  2. `|m| > 5.0 pp`;
  3. the **block-bootstrap 95% CI excludes 0** *and* the **episode-clustered 95% CI excludes 0** (DP-51). An endpoint clearing the MPE on the block CI but not on the episode CI is **INCONCLUSIVE, never CONFIRMED**;
  4. BH **q ≤ 0.10** within the question (m = 2) **and** across F6's 17 primaries (§7), computed on the within-night permutation p;
  5. the estimate has the **same sign in both halves**, neither half beyond MPE in the opposite sign; **no tape stratum with ≥ 20 contributing nights beyond MPE in the opposite sign**, and the sign held in **every** tape stratum clearing 20 nights; and the same two conditions in every **`dam5` tercile** clearing 20 nights — the "is this the tier, or is it how far the stock had already fallen" clause;
  6. **the monotonicity gate:** on the ≥ 20 three-group nights, both adjacent contrasts (`G_B − G_A` and `G_C − G_B`) carry **the same sign as the extreme contrast**, and **neither is beyond MPE in the opposite sign**. No MPE is required of the individual steps. **If there are fewer than 20 three-group nights the gate is not evaluable and the verdict is INCONCLUSIVE**, never CONFIRMED on the extreme contrast alone: a two-group result is not the calibration claim H-097 makes;
  7. **B3 (`S2`) does not contradict it:** if the within-published damage-paired contrast clears 20 contributing nights **and** is beyond MPE in the **opposite** sign, the verdict is INCONCLUSIVE. B3 being thin, absent or merely below MPE blocks nothing, and B3 can **never** create a CONFIRMED (§11 decision 3);
  8. **the damage-matched form is the verdict.** If the matched estimate is inside MPE while B4's unmatched ordering is beyond it, the endpoint is **NULL for the Conviction Monitor** and the report says so in those words: the tiers added nothing to what the fall already said (DP-12).
- **Both primaries must be CONFIRMED in the concordant sign** (`m1 > 0` with `m2 < 0`, or both reversed) **before any rule follows** (§9). P1 confirmed with P2 NULL is a statement about which level is reached first with no statement about reaching the favourable level at all; either mixed case is reported as **split**, not as a partial win.
- **NULL:** floors met **and both 95% CIs lie entirely inside (−5.0 pp, +5.0 pp)** — an equivalence rule, not "the CI includes zero". This is the only reading under which the desk may say the displayed tiers do not order the path. **§5.4 records that this needs ≈ 320 contributing nights and is unlikely to be reachable on this window**; it is registered here rather than discovered at the decision pass, and no floor is lowered to manufacture it.
- **INCONCLUSIVE:** anything else — halves disagreeing in sign; a tape stratum or a `dam5` tercile beyond MPE in the opposite sign; clause 6 failing or not evaluable; clause 7 contradicting; `0 < |m| ≤ 5.0 pp` with a CI excluding 0; a CI including 0 with `|m| ≥ 5.0 pp`; or the two CIs disagreeing (clause 3). A SUPPRESSED sub-cell is not by itself INCONCLUSIVE. **A gate shortfall is not INCONCLUSIVE either** — it fires §5.6's single DP-13 extension, then DEFERRED.

## 10. Known threats to validity (registrar's own list)

1. **The grouping variable is a function of the path, so the naive ordering is a momentum ordering.** The dominant threat, and the reason B2 matches on `dam5`, §8 clause 5 requires the sign inside every `dam5` tercile and clause 8 makes the matched form the verdict. Residual risk: `dam5` measures the damage at one instant in ATR and not *how* the name got there (one gap versus five drifting sessions); the tercile cells, the SMD panel and B4 are the reads on what is left.
2. **Group composition differs in damage, so the excess contrast can still carry a composition effect** even though each group is netted against its own damage-matched controls — if the damage-matched excess is itself a function of damage level. The `dam5`-tercile clause in §8.5 is the protection; B3 (§3) is the within-published version and is one-way by §11 decision 3.
3. **Powered for a large effect only.** MDE ≈ 11–14 pp against a 5 pp MPE; NULL as an equivalence claim is probably unreachable on this window (§5.4). A true ordering of 5–12 pp returns INCONCLUSIVE, which §9 forbids reading as "the monitor is useless".
4. **HOLD is unreachable and the monitor is one-armed (PI-014).** `G_A` is "WATCH throughout", not "conviction intact". Every statement inherits that (§1, §8). A **dated** polarity restoration inside the window makes the tier a different feature and fires §5.6's window-start rule, with DEFERRED live past 2027-09-15; an undated recovery moves nothing and is printed by month (R2 clause vi).
5. **The flow input behind the dead arm is itself truncated (PI-020).** Even a restored polarity arm would be computed from a decomposition that drops puts on the most liquid names. The verdict is about the monitor as built; §9 forbids generalising it to a repaired one.
6. **`G_B` is partly an artefact of the age decay, not a repair.** Days 3–5 are shown one step softer (`:50-56`, `:807-811`), so a "softening" a subscriber sees can be the clock rather than the chart. This question classifies on the **undecayed** `overall_tier` precisely to avoid building the decay into the partition (§2.3, §11 decision 1), and the displayed-field partition and the day-5 cross-tab are printed (§4 secondaries 3) so the two readings are always visible.
7. **The monitor's window is five weekdays, not five sessions,** and the job is cron'd Mon–Fri. Across a holiday a pick has four in-scope rows; §2.3's ≥ 4-row rule keeps those picks and the count of 4-row picks is printed, with the terminal state one session older than the basis (earlier, hence legal).
8. **Hourly bars are missing for unpublished controls on the sealed portion** (227 of 436 symbols), so DP-27 resolves every same-session tie on the control side as adverse-first. The bias is common to all three groups and cancels to first order in a difference of excesses; it is printed per group and per arm, the opposite reading is a registered sensitivity, and R2 requests hourly bars for all candidate symbols on the successor freezes.
9. **Monitor rows are delete-and-reinsert and the entry point accepts an arbitrary `--trading-date`** (`:1097-1101`; `scripts/run_conviction_monitor.py:38-62`). The §2.5 point-in-time guard is the defence; measured in-window it drops nothing (0 failures June–September). Under DP-50 a future repair could rewrite monitor history; nights ≤ 2026-09-10 are pinned and safe by rule 4.
10. **A falling tape produces both more EXIT tiers and more adverse-first races.** §8 clause 5's tape-stratum requirement is the protection, and nothing here speaks to the strong April–May tape, which is outside the window.
11. **Overlapping 10-session windows and repeated symbols** inflate precision. The 8-session block bootstrap, the DP-51 episode bootstrap (a **gate**, not a footnote) and the within-night permutation p are the three registered protections.
12. **Control attrition is unmeasured** and is the main feasibility risk (§5.3). The 0.29/session pre-lock trigger sends the question to DEFERRED rather than to a widened caliper or a switched pool; **the caliper is never widened at the decision pass**.
13. **Design exposure to counts.** The three-group structure and its expected sizes come from a counts-only census (§6). No outcome was seen, but the design is not blind to the *shape* of the tier distribution; the honest statement is this line, and the sealed nights stay in the window because the outcome side is untouched.
14. **First touch is not money.** No stop is assumed and no exit is taken (rule 5, DP-02); the already-held plan panel (§4 secondary 10) is the only place a monetary reading is permitted, and it is descriptive.
