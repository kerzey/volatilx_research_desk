## Standing rules proposed
- A **companion BH correction** is required whenever a new question's primary endpoint is **byte-identical** to a locked question's in another family (same metric, same clock, same anchor, overlapping nights), and the **larger of the two q-values is the one quoted**. Q012 and Q015 invented this case by case; stated once it stops a question being placed in the family with the smallest correction.
- When the conditioning variable is a **function of the option chain rather than of the stock** (pin risk, chain density, spacing), the matched control is matched on **price level as well as volatility**, because strike spacing in percent is mechanically a price-level variable; and the control-adjusted contrast carries a **hard** gate rather than an opposite-sign gate, because the confound is mechanical rather than speculative.
- A question whose **feature is read from a snapshot column the platform may later repair** (`context_json.gex_context` here, `uoa_symbol_daily` in Q014) names the repair constraint **in its own §9 PI filing** at lock: flag-off until the decision date, or the column is two features split at the ship date. This is DP-50(a)/(b) applied forward, at registration, instead of being discovered when a successor freeze disagrees with v001.
