## Standing rules proposed (for Haci to confirm later)
- **A census, not a sample, is the desk's knowledge-time standard, and the two run together.** The point-in-time register runs from a byte-identical script at every freeze and the 20-row hand sample is kept beside it as the check on the script itself. (Item 3; generalises to every freeze.)
- **A live query never classifies a study row, not even for provenance.** Where the frozen parquet and the read-only platform repo at the frozen SHA cannot settle a classification, it stays unsettled and the question reports it as unresolved. (Item 4; generalises to every diagnostic; it is rule 4 and DP-50(c) applied, so it may not need a DP id of its own.)
- **A "verified reconstruction" needs its tolerance and its failure branch written before the run.** Otherwise "verified" is decided while looking. (Item 2; generalises to every question that recovers a point-in-time value from a mutated column.)
- **A re-run loop is bounded at registration.** A pass that may be fixed and re-run carries a maximum number of passes and a hard stop, or it is a look. (Item 7; generalises to every gated diagnostic.)
- **`available_time_et: "16:05"` labels the nightly batch of night N, not a literal clock.** A write is late when it lands on a **later ET calendar day** than the night it is attributed to; the batch's write latency is the time taken to persist the 16:05 state, not the arrival of post-16:05 information. Anything later than that — a next-morning pass, an overnight job, a re-run — is post-decision, whatever the next session's open says. (A1, 2026-09-14; generalises to every freeze config, every KT audit and every future register.)
- **A rule whose verdict is fixed before any data is read is a specification defect, not a finding.** Where a pre-registered reading makes one branch unreachable by construction, the desk fixes the reading by dated amendment before the run and prints the alternatives as a descriptive sensitivity, rather than reporting the foregone verdict. (A1 / A1r, 2026-09-14; generalises to every diagnostic with a PASS/FAIL branch.)
