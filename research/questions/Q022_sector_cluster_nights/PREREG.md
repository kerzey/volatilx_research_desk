## 1. Hypothesis (plain English, one sentence)

**On nights when the engine publishes a concentrated slate — four or more of the eight picks from the same sector — the picks' price paths after selection are different from the paths on diversified nights, measured as the same picks' excess over same-night look-alike stocks carrying a target at the identical distance.**

Plain version of why it matters: on a concentrated night a subscriber who takes all eight picks does not own eight ideas, he owns something closer to one idea eight times. The platform prints nothing about this today. Either the concentration is a warning (the slate is one bet and the per-pick edge is no better, so the correct response is to size down or take fewer names), or it is a signal (the engine converging on one sector is the engine finding a real theme, and the correct response is to take the slate as printed). Both are actionable and the desk does not know which is true.

**Registered two-sided and with no predicted direction.** H-034 states the contrast and no sign. Nothing in the desk's sealed outputs measures it (§6). A result beyond MPE with either sign is a confirmed finding carrying that sign.

**Why this is registrable now when EXPLORE_001 said it was not.** EXPLORE_001 §8 recorded "Sector cluster nights (H-034): not testable from this freeze (industry 12% populated)", and PI-007 is filed on exactly that. That conclusion is about the **`industry` column**, and it is correct about it: `industry` is populated from the FMP fundamentals profile only when fundamental enrichment is on (volatilx `services/symbol_context_builder.py:525`), it is descriptive and feeds no scoring, and its two sibling columns `industry_rotation_active` / `industry_rotation_bonus` are dead and always NULL (volatilx `docs/SUPER_AGENT_SELECT_PROCESS.md` §8.1). There is **no `sector` column in the frozen `sas_candidates` table** — the migration that would have added one is archived (`scripts/archive/migrate_sas_sector_columns.py:27-31`) and its columns were renamed to `industry*` before the bonus itself was deleted in platform commit `66cb63d`. But the platform carries a **second, independent and nearly complete** symbol→sector artifact that the desk had not looked at: `data/sp500_sectors.json`, a committed symbol → SPDR sector-ETF mapping over the S&P 500 merged with the Russell 2000 (`scripts/bootstrap_sp500_sectors.py:11`, `:37-55`), read in production by `services/sector_breadth.py:78-80` and by the bear exemplar library builder. It is **dated 2026-05-17** (`_meta.last_synced`), which is seven weeks before the first pick night in this question's window, so using it involves no look-ahead of any kind (§6, rule 14). Its coverage of the candidate universe was the gate on whether this question runs at all, and it was **measured before lock** (§5): **99.5% of distinct candidate symbols and 99.5–99.6% of published rows** are in the pinned mapping, the only two unmapped symbols in the entire frozen history are **BRK.B** and **BF.B**, and **no night in the report period was UNDETERMINED**. The gate is passed on measured counts, not on expectation.

### 2.3 Night eligibility and the arm label — computed at 16:05 ET, before any forward bar is read

For each candidate night `t` in the window:

1. Drop the night entirely if it appears in **`research/data/exclusions_v003.json` unioned with the add-only successor exclusions file issued with `manifest_v002`** (`manual_runs.trading_dates` ∪ `non_session_runs.trading_dates` ∪ `uncorroborated_publication_runs.trading_dates`, the identical three lists built by the identical criteria in both files) or if `finished_at` is later than the next session's open (**DP-04**). The union is what applies, not v003 alone: this window runs four to five months past v003's coverage, and leaving the post-freeze nights to DP-04 alone would silently drop the `uncorroborated_publication_runs` and `non_session_runs` protections the sealed nights get. The successor file may only **add** nights; no night is ever removed from a v003 list; the **criteria** are fixed at this lock even though the **dates** cannot be. `eval.py` reads both JSONs as inputs; **no date and no file name is hard-coded** (DP-22, DECISIONS item 7).
2. Let `k_t` = the number of published picks on night `t`. **A night enters the primary only if `k_t = 8`** (the literal denominator of H-034's "≥4 of 8"; DP-25, and §10.10 for the measured cost). Nights with `k_t ≠ 8` are counted by value of `k_t` — **both tails**, since `k_t > 8` nights are measured to exist and are the larger tail — reported in the funnel, and they enter **no arm**. This is not tidiness: with an *absolute* cut of four, a short slate is mechanically pushed toward DIVERSIFIED and a long one toward CLUSTER, so a mixed denominator would confound the arm with slate size. **A `k_t ≥ 6` primary, or a relative cut (`m_t / k_t ≥ 0.5`), is a successor hypothesis and is never a switch at the decision pass** (DP-25; DECISIONS item 2).
3. Map each of the eight symbols through the pinned mapping. Let `u_t` = the number with no mapping, and `m_t` = the largest number of *mapped* picks sharing one sector ETF. **The unmapped set is whatever the pinned blob does not contain, on every night including post-lock ones, and the desk never hand-maps, supplements or overrides it** (DECISIONS item 13). Measured over the whole frozen history the unmapped set is exactly **BRK.B and BF.B** — dual-class tickers whose period-suffix format the mapping's bootstrap script does not carry — published BRK.B ×3 (two in-window), all-candidate BRK.B ×8 / BF.B ×7; they are counted in `u_t`, listed by name with their appearance counts in the funnel, and a night they push to UNDETERMINED is counted and reported, **never rescued**. Three reasons, each sufficient: a desk-side supplement breaks the sha256 pin that makes the study reproducible (§2.1); it would be a classification rule invented after seeing which symbols failed, which is a judgment in the inner loop (rule 9); and the bias is already one-directional and disclosed (§10.3), so leaving them unmapped is the conservative side. The correct response to the gap is a platform enhancement (§9), never a desk-side patch inside the study.
4. **Arm label, deterministic and exhaustive:**
   - **CLUSTER** iff `m_t ≥ 4`. (Unmapped symbols cannot overturn this: they can only add to a group.)
   - **DIVERSIFIED** iff `m_t + u_t ≤ 3`. (Even if every unmapped symbol joined the largest group, the night would still be under the cut.)
   - **UNDETERMINED** otherwise — excluded from every arm, counted, and reported by night with `m_t` and `u_t`. The asymmetry is deliberate and is disclosed: missing coverage can never manufacture a CLUSTER night, only cost a DIVERSIFIED one, so mapping gaps bias the arm mix and not the label (§10.3).
   - `dominant_sector_t` = the ETF attaining `m_t` (ties broken by ETF ticker ascending), recorded for every CLUSTER night.
5. **Contributing night (the floor unit, DP-21):** a night that is CLUSTER or DIVERSIFIED, is matured (§5), and carries **≥ 5 gradeable picks with a valid control set** after §2.4. A night that survives to here but yields no gradeable pick is a **non-contributing night, not an exclusion** — printed in the funnel, never added to any exclusions file.

### 2.4 Pick-level exclusions (measurement failures only; each counted)

- No swing-lane target in `public_payload_json.lane_plans` (the known null-ladder denominator; PI-006) → excluded, counted.
- `outcome_target_invalid` non-null, or the swing t1 on the wrong side of `C_t` for the pick's direction → excluded, counted.
- **Price-scale screen (DATA_NOTES 2026-09-13):** `target / C_t` outside `[0.5, 2.0]` for a swing t1, or an implied ATR distance above 12 ATR → excluded, counted, and the symbol listed. This is the APH / KLAC / CRWD / MNST signature; it is not assumed to be limited to those four.
- Fewer than 60 daily bars dated ≤ t in `prices_daily_split` (ATR14, `beta60`, `runup20` undefined), or no `C_t`, or no session t+1 open → excluded, counted.
- **Ungradeable:** a pick is gradeable if either every session t+1..t+20 has a daily bar, or the touch is already determined by the bars that exist. Anything else (halt, delisting, acquisition mid-window with no touch) → excluded, counted. This is a measurement failure and can never move a night between arms, because the arm is fixed at the pick-night close.
- Fewer than **3** valid matched controls (§3 B1) → the pick is dropped from the primary and counted (the Q013 §3 rule, reused).
- Immature nights — session t+20 after the last trading date of the pinned price freeze — are **excluded and counted**, never graded as a non-touch. Maturity comes from the trading calendar, never from whether a bar happens to exist.

**Measured cost of these screens on the sealed period** (R1(g), 550 published picks, counts only): no swing-lane target **8 (1.45%)**; price-scale screen **19 of 542 (3.5%)** — **APH 15**, CRWD 2, KLAC 1, MNST 1, reproducing the DATA_NOTES signature exactly; `outcome_target_invalid` **4**; fewer than 60 prior bars / no `C_t` / no session t+1 open **0**. **The price-scale screen stays symbol-agnostic as drafted — it is a rule, not that list**, and it applies unchanged to post-lock nights. B1's ≥ 3-control drop is not binding anywhere in the sealed data (100% of picks retain ≥ 3; §3).

## 3. Baseline(s) — what this must beat

H-034 as written is "cluster nights vs diversified nights", which names its own arm baseline. That alone would not be a rule-5 test, because both arms are published picks in the same tape and a raw difference between them is a statement about which stocks got picked, not about what the picking was worth. So there are three baselines and the primary uses two of them at once.

- **B1 — the rule-5 distance-matched control, inside the endpoint.** For each eligible pick `p` on night `t`, the **10 nearest same-night non-published `sas_candidates` rows** (the complement of DP-28's predicate, dark-lane rows included) on `beta60` / `atr_pct` / `runup20` computed from `prices_daily_split` bars dated ≤ t, standardized by the night's cross-sectional median and MAD, Euclidean distance, with replacement across picks, ties by symbol ascending — **the Q006 §3 construction reused verbatim**, so control cohorts are comparable across questions. Each control carries a **synthetic target at the identical ATR distance and the same direction**: `d_p = dir_p × (L3_p − C_{t,p}) / ATR_p`, applied to the control's own close and its own ATR14, graded on the control's own session t+1 open over the same 20 sessions. Controls **never add to inferential n** (rule 6). B1 absorbs the night, the tape, and the volatility profile of the names — which is what makes the arm contrast a statement about *selection* on concentrated nights rather than about the market. **Measured (R1(f)): B1 is not binding anywhere** — 100% of the 550 sealed-period picks retain ≥ 3 matched controls, pool sizes 37–60 (median 53).
- **B2 — the arm baseline, which is what H-034 names: DIVERSIFIED nights.** Same window, same publication process, same 20-session clock, same control construction. This is "Y's rate when X did not happen, in the same regime", and the primary estimand is the difference of B1-adjusted rates between the two arms.
- **B3 — the sector-matched control (a gate, §8 clause 7, not a primary).** Identical to B1 except that the neighbour pool for pick `p` is first restricted to same-night unpublished candidates carrying the **same sector ETF** as `p`, then the nearest **10 (minimum 3)** are taken on the same three features. B3 is the instrument that separates the two readings of any CLUSTER result: if concentrated nights look worse merely because the concentrated sector itself went nowhere, B3's contrast collapses; if the engine's selection is genuinely weaker when it concentrates, B3's contrast survives. B3 conditions on nothing that happens after the pick night. **Measured (R1(e)): B3 is projected evaluable but is expressly NOT pre-certified** — in-window, **80.9%** of picks keep ≥ 3 same-sector controls (CLUSTER 83.1% / DIVERSIFIED 78.3%) and **31 of 32** nights retain ≥ 50% of their picks (CLUSTER 17/17, DIVERSIFIED 14/15), median B3 pool 7. §8 clause 7's EVALUABLE test reads `eval.py`'s **measured counts at the decision pass** and **is not weakened, pre-certified or converted into a projection**; §10.8's thin-sector risk stands.
- **B4 — the unconditional rates, descriptive.** Pooled published touch rate, pooled control touch rate, and the arm contrast with *no* control adjustment. B4 is what stops a 60%-ish touch rate from reading as a finding on its own.
- **Label permutation (the p-value's null):** the CLUSTER/DIVERSIFIED label permuted across nights within calendar month, preserving each month's arm counts — 10,000 draws, seed 20260913 (§4).

## 4. Objective metric (rule 5 — price path, measured the way it is traded)

**Entry basis: the official 09:30 ET open of session t+1** (DP-03(b), DP-42) — for published picks and for every control, one basis on both sides. The decision this question informs is taken at publication and acted on at the next open; the pick-night close basis (DP-11's construction) is computed and printed as a named sensitivity and decides nothing.

**Primary level: L3** — `public_payload_json.lane_plans.swing_trading.targets[0]`, the first swing target, direction-aware (DP-42: the hypothesis names no level, so the swing-lane default applies). **Primary clock: first touch within sessions t+1..t+20 from the entry** (DP-09 — the 20-session spread horizon decides; the platform's own 40-session swing window `services/sas_conviction_card.py:194` is printed alongside, descriptively, wherever it has matured).

**Touch rules, applied by `eval.py` with no judgment:**
1. `HIT_p = 1` iff some session `s ∈ [t+1, t+20]` has `high_s ≥ L3_p` (bullish) or `low_s ≤ L3_p` (bearish).
2. **A target already at or through the session t+1 open is not a hit** (DP-03, DP-26): such a pick is graded `HIT = 0`, flagged `passed_at_entry`, and the primaries are additionally recomputed with those picks removed from both arms as a named sensitivity. The identical rule applies to every control's synthetic target.
3. A gap through the level during t+1..t+20 is a touch.
4. No stop is assumed anywhere and no touch is an exit (rule 5, DP-02). Adverse excursion and the counter-direction touches are reported (§4 secondaries), never used as exits.

**Per-pick excess, per night, per arm:**

- `e_p = HIT_p − mean(HIT over p's matched control set)` — in percentage points, per pick.
- `E_t = mean_p e_p` over the night's eligible picks — **one number per night, which is the unit of inference** (rule 6).

**Primary endpoint (one, pre-specified):**

> **E1 = mean over CLUSTER nights of `E_t` − mean over DIVERSIFIED nights of `E_t`**, in percentage points. Two-sided.

**How a within-night control works when the arm is a property of the whole night.** The arm cannot be assigned within a night — every pick on a cluster night is on a cluster night — so the contrast that decides is necessarily *between* nights, and between-night contrasts are where the tape does its damage. The construction above splits the problem in two: the **within-night** half (`e_p`) is a paired comparison against stocks that lived through the same session with a target at the same ATR distance, which removes the night's market move, the night's volatility level and the night's candidate pool; the **between-night** half (E1) then compares two arms of *already tape-adjusted* numbers. So the quantity that decides is never "cluster nights happened to be good days" — a night on which everything ran gives `E_t ≈ 0` just like a night on which nothing did. This is also why E1 is a difference of differences and carries the doubled MPE (§8).

**Registered secondaries — printed always, deciding never:**

- **The B3 sector-matched version of E1** (§3, §8 clause 7), with its own control-set sizes, its EVALUABLE counts, and the share of picks and nights it retains.
- **The B4 raw arm contrast:** mean night-level `HIT` share, CLUSTER minus DIVERSIFIED, with no control adjustment — the number a naive look would produce, printed so the adjustment's size is visible.
- **Dose–response in the concentration count:** `E_t` by `m_t ∈ {2, 3} / {4} / {5} / {6+}`, and against the night's sector **HHI** (`Σ share²` over the eight picks) as a continuous alternative. **SUPPRESSED at lock (§5, DECISIONS item 14): `m_t = {5}` and `m_t = {6+}`** — each projects ≈ 9 contributing nights against the 20 floor. `{2,3}` (≈ 43) and `{4}` (≈ 32) clear, so **the dose–response is reportable as a three-point shape at most and never as a monotone ladder over six cells**. A monotone dose–response would be the strongest available evidence that the arm is real; it decides nothing (DP-44: sessions-to-touch and shape statistics are descriptive, and no MPE is invented for an HHI slope).
- **Co-movement and dispersion (the "eight picks are one bet" claim):** per night, the share of picks hitting L3, its between-night variance by arm, the all-or-nothing rate (share = 0 or 1), and the mean pairwise correlation of per-pick 20-session returns within a night. **Descriptive by DP-44** — the desk has no MPE for a dispersion endpoint and does not invent one — and §9 forbids any rule resting on it alone.
- **Dominant-sector breakdown:** the distribution of `dominant_sector_t` over CLUSTER nights, E1 recomputed on XLK-dominant nights only, and the dominant sector ETF's own 20-session forward return printed beside each cluster night as context. The ETF return is a post-entry quantity and is never a filter, a match or a stratum. **SUPPRESSED at lock (§5, DECISIONS item 14): every non-XLK `dominant_sector_t` cell** — XLF projects ≈ 9, XLV ≈ 3, and the other eight SPDR sectors had **zero** observed CLUSTER nights. **XLK (≈ 37) is the only reportable dominant-sector cell**, and there is therefore **no "non-XLK" panel**: the complement is a suppressed aggregate of suppressed cells and is printed as counts only.
- **The rest of the ladder and the counter side:** first touch of L1, L2, L4 on their own lane windows, the counter-direction levels and the printed swing stop (`sas_selection_excursion.counter_touch_dates_json` style, recomputed by the desk from bars, never read from the outcome table), the order in which levels were hit, MFE and MAE in ATR from the entry, and sessions-to-first-touch.
- **The 40-session L3 result**, wherever matured (DP-09).
- **The ±1.5 ATR synthetic-level version of E1**, in which the target is placed at a fixed ATR distance from `C_t` instead of at the printed L3 — the read on §10.5, registered here so it can never be promoted at the decision pass.
- **Close-to-close return** at T+20 from the entry, by arm. **Fixed-horizon return is descriptive by rule 5 and DP-01 and never decides**, at any point, in any branch.
- Sub-cells: bull/bear, score band (80–85 / 85–90 / 90+), `best_timeframe` lane, tape stratum, regime label — **not pre-suppressed at lock and not loose either**: R1 did not break these out, and pre-suppressing a cell the desk has not counted would be an invented number, so they fall to the standing rule — **reported only at ≥ 20 measured contributing nights, SUPPRESSED below, counts printed either way**, strictly binding in every branch. The tape and regime strata cannot be pre-suppressed even in principle, since the expanding-window terciles are defined by sessions that have not happened. R2 returns their funnel counts as **information for the report, not a gate** (§5).

**What SUPPRESSED means, fixed at lock (DECISIONS items 6 and 14).** Counts only — no point estimate, no sign, no "small n, directionally", no q-value. A cell suppressed at lock **stays suppressed even if it clears 20 measured nights at the decision pass**. Suppression restricts **affirmative reporting only**: it never removes a cell from §8 clause 6 (a tape or regime cell with ≥ 20 contributing nights beyond MPE in the opposite sign still blocks CONFIRMED), never removes it from clause 7, and never by itself makes E1 INCONCLUSIVE. **UNDETERMINED is an empty cell, not a suppressed one** — 0 nights measured over the whole report period, printed as a measured zero.

**Quotability.** The primary is a 20-session window and is therefore **NON_QUOTABLE** under rule 12 in every branch; the W60 ladder companion is the only quotable basis here, and nothing is quotable at all before PROSPECTIVELY_CONFIRMED (rule 10).

**Inference (rule 6).**
- **p-value (decides significance):** permutation of the night arm labels **within calendar month**, preserving each month's CLUSTER / DIVERSIFIED counts, 10,000 permutations, seed 20260913. Month-stratification is what stops the test from rewarding a run of concentrated nights that happened to fall in a good month.
- **CI (decides the excludes-zero clause):** stationary block bootstrap over the ordered contributing nights, **expected block length 10 sessions** (half the 20-session forward window — the Q011 / Q015 convention, fixed here and not chosen after seeing results), 2,000 resamples. The date-clustered bootstrap CI is printed alongside and is expected to be narrower; **the block-bootstrap interval decides.**
- **Symbol-overlap sensitivity:** distinct symbols per arm, the repeat-selection count per symbol, a leave-one-symbol-out jackknife on E1, and E1 recomputed with each symbol's per-arm contribution capped at its median night count. Descriptive.
- Every estimate prints: n(contributing nights, per arm), n(contributing nights dated after the lock commit, per arm), n(eligible picks), n(HIT), n(`passed_at_entry`), n(control rows and control-set sizes), n(UNDETERMINED nights), n(`k_t ≠ 8` nights, by `k_t`), n(ungradeable), n(immature), n(excluded, by reason), the mapping coverage, and the post-match standardized mean differences for B1 and B3.

## 5. Sample floors and expected n

- **Floors (rule 6 as read by DP-21):** ≥ **80 contributing nights on the primary endpoint** and ≥ **20 contributing nights per arm** and per reported sub-cell. A sub-cell below 20 is **SUPPRESSED** (counts only; not "small n, directionally") and does not by itself make the endpoint INCONCLUSIVE. **An arm below 20 is different and is not demotable:** E1 *is* the arm contrast, so an arm short of the floor leaves no endpoint — it fires the gate below (the single DP-13 extension, then DEFERRED), and it is never demoted to descriptive and never read as INCONCLUSIVE (§8). ## 8. Decision rule (numeric, written before unsealing)

`E1` is in **percentage points of L3-touch rate within 20 sessions, control-adjusted, CLUSTER minus DIVERSIFIED**, over the contributing nights of §2.3.

**MPE: ±10.0 pp.** DP-20's standing number for a control-adjusted or within-night touch-rate endpoint is +5.0 pp, and DP-20 permits the Registrar to propose a **larger** MPE with a one-line reason. The reason: **E1 is a difference of differences** — an arm contrast of two already control-adjusted rates — and the desk's standing treatment of a difference of differences is to double the MPE of its components (Q012 P2 at 0.50 ATR against DP-10's 0.25; Q018 P4 on the same grounds). A doubled MPE is also the only honest one at this n: with ~80 nights the standard error of E1 is of the same order as 5 pp, so a 5 pp bar would produce INCONCLUSIVE by construction rather than by evidence. **The MPE is never lowered at the decision pass, in any branch — including one in which the 10.0 pp bar turns a CI-excludes-zero result into INCONCLUSIVE.** The same 10.0 pp governs clause 7's B3 gate below (DECISIONS item 5; Q007 §8, Q014 §8 E1, Q012 P2, Q018 P4 are the desk's standing treatment of a difference of differences).

The endpoint is two-sided and the verdict carries its sign.

- **HISTORICALLY_CONFIRMED** requires **all** of:
  1. **≥ 80 contributing nights**, **≥ 20 in each arm**, and **≥ 30 contributing nights dated after the lock commit** (DP-21, DP-24) — plus **≥ 20 contributing nights for any sub-cell that is reported**; a sub-cell below floor is **SUPPRESSED** (counts only, no point estimate, no sign, no q-value) and does not by itself make the endpoint INCONCLUSIVE. The cells suppressed **at lock** are fixed in §5 and stay suppressed **even if they clear 20 measured nights at the pass**; suppression restricts affirmative reporting only and never removes a cell from clause 6 or clause 7;
  2. `|E1| > 10.0 pp`;
  3. the **block-bootstrap** 95% CI (§4) excludes 0;
  4. BH **q ≤ 0.10** across F4's 10 primaries, computed on the within-month label-permutation p (m = 1 within the question, so the within-question correction is the identity);
  5. the point estimate has the **same sign in both halves** (§6), and neither half is beyond MPE in the opposite sign;
  6. no tape stratum and no regime cell with ≥ 20 contributing nights is beyond MPE in the opposite sign;
  7. **the B3 sector-matched contrast has the same sign as E1 and `|E1_B3| ≥ 10.0 pp`** — the "is this concentration, or is it the sector?" gate (DP-45, the Q021 clause-7 pattern; point estimate only, no CI requirement). **EVALUABLE is defined numerically and measured, not judged:** B3 is evaluable iff **each arm retains ≥ 20 contributing nights** under the B3 pools **and ≥ 50% of the eligible picks on those nights keep ≥ 3 same-sector controls**. Individual picks lost to a thin same-sector pool are dropped and counted, never imputed. **The gate reads `eval.py`'s measured counts at the decision pass and is neither pre-certified nor converted into a projection:** R1 measured B3 as projected evaluable (80.9% of in-window picks keep ≥ 3 same-sector controls; 31 of 32 nights retain ≥ 50% of their picks — §3), and **a projected-feasible gate is not a passed gate** (DP-45). **If B3 is not EVALUABLE, the verdict is INCONCLUSIVE, never CONFIRMED on B1 alone** — a concentrated slate is, before anything else, a bet on one sector, and the desk will not license a rule that cannot tell the two apart.
- **NULL:** floors met **and** the 95% CI includes 0 **and** `|E1| < 10.0 pp`. "Whether the night's slate is concentrated does not change what an individual pick does, relative to look-alikes" is a real and useful finding — it retires the "eight picks are one bet" worry from the *selection-quality* column and moves it entirely into the *sizing* column, where the descriptive dispersion table (§4) is what informs it. It is ledgered with the same care as a confirmation.
- **INCONCLUSIVE:** anything else — halves disagreeing in sign; a tape stratum or regime cell beyond MPE in the opposite sign; the B3 gate failing or not evaluable; `0 < |E1| ≤ 10.0 pp` with the CI excluding 0 ("real but below MPE", rule 6); or the CI including 0 with `|E1| ≥ 10.0 pp`. **A gate shortfall is not INCONCLUSIVE:** it fires the single DP-13 extension, then DEFERRED (§5).
- **What a confirmed endpoint does and does not mean (binding on the report).** A CONFIRMED-negative E1 means **"on nights when four or more of the eight picks came from one sector, the average pick beat its same-night, same-ATR-distance look-alikes by at least 10 percentage points less than on diversified nights"**. It does **not** mean cluster nights lose money, that the picks fell, or that the sector was wrong — no stop and no exit exists anywhere in this question (DP-02), and the fixed-horizon return never decides. A CONFIRMED-positive E1 means the mirror statement and is reported with its sign. Neither licenses any sentence about *which* sector, unless the dominant-sector breakdown clears its own 20-night floor, and even then it is descriptive.

## 10. Known threats to validity (registrar's own list)

1. **The arm is not random — and the "almost constant" version of this threat is now measured and closed as a feasibility matter.** The published slate is drawn from a candidate pool that is itself concentrated; the desk's standing note is that the elite picks are "mostly memory/semis". If nearly every night were CLUSTER (or nearly none), the contrast would have little variation to work with. **Measured before lock, not discovered after: 17 CLUSTER / 15 DIVERSIFIED on the 32 `k_t = 8` nights of the in-window period, 28/26 over 2026-06-01..2026-09-10, UNDETERMINED 0**; the pre-window split (11/11, informational) agrees. The arm is real and it varies. What remains is not feasibility but **interpretation** — threat 2.
2. **CLUSTER is partly a statement about the tape, the protections may not be enough, and this is now the question's live threat — the one thing R1 could not measure.** Concentrated slates plausibly appear when one sector is running, so arm and tape are correlated by construction. Three things push back — the within-night control (which nets out the night's market move), the month-stratified permutation, and the B3 sector-matched gate — and none of them is a randomization. With **XLK the only reportable dominant-sector cell** (§5), a large part of "CLUSTER" in this window is likely to mean "technology", and the B3 gate is what must carry that. If the window turns out to be one tape with one dominant sector, rule 7's "a result that only holds in one period is not a result" bites and the honest verdict is INCONCLUSIVE. The report says this plainly beside the numbers.
3. **Mapping coverage was the question's single point of failure; it is measured, its bias is one-directional, and the gap is never patched.** A night with unmapped symbols can still be certified CLUSTER but not DIVERSIFIED (§2.3), so coverage loss thins the DIVERSIFIED arm specifically — the bias can cost a DIVERSIFIED night and can never manufacture a CLUSTER one. **Measured: 99.5–99.9% coverage, two unmapped symbols in the entire frozen history (BRK.B, BF.B), exactly one `k_t = 8` night with `u_t = 1`, none with `u_t ≥ 2`, UNDETERMINED = 0** — the measured cost is nil. The disclosure stands unchanged and carries the **no-hand-map rule** of §2.3 step 3: the desk does not supplement the pinned blob even for an obvious formatting bug, because that would break the sha256 pin, would be a rule invented after seeing which symbols failed (rule 9), and would move the bias off its disclosed conservative side. The mapping is also **stale by construction** — one commit, 2026-05-17, deliberately frozen there — so a reclassification during the window mislabels a symbol. Reclassifications run ~5–10 a year across the S&P, small against an eight-name slate, and staleness cannot leak the future.
4. **The mapping is a repo file, not a database table.** It was built from FMP company profiles by a one-shot script and has no per-date history. The desk pins one version by sha256 and `eval.py` fails on a mismatch, so the *study* is reproducible; the underlying classification's accuracy is inherited from FMP and is not audited here.
5. **L3 is where the platform prints it, not where volatility says it should be.** PI-009 found the ladder is distance, not forecast; the printed levels carried a mechanism change this window is placed entirely **after** (`_enforce_ladder_monotonic`, shipped 2026-07-06 15:13:42 ET — handled by §6's start rule, measured) and a price-scale defect for some symbols (handled by §2.4's screen, measured at 19 of 542). The distance-matched control absorbs most of what remains: a badly placed L3 is placed equally badly for the control, at the identical ATR distance. The ±1.5 ATR synthetic-level version (§4) is registered as the read on this and can never be promoted to primary.
6. **Overlapping 20-session windows and repeat symbols.** Adjacent nights share most of their forward sessions and frequently the same symbols, so night counts overstate independent information. The block bootstrap (10-session blocks), the within-month permutation, and the leave-one-symbol-out jackknife are the pre-specified protections; they reduce the problem and do not remove it, and the direction of the residual error is toward over-narrow CIs, i.e. toward false confirmation. That is the reason for the doubled MPE and the B3 gate, not merely for the bootstrap choice.
7. **A difference of differences is noisier than either difference.** With one primary, ~80 nights and a 10 pp bar, this question is powered to find a large effect or nothing. INCONCLUSIVE is a likely outcome and is the acceptable direction of error; it is not evidence of absence and the report must not present it as such.
8. **B3's pools will be thin outside XLK.** A utilities pick on a June night may have no same-sector unpublished look-alike at all. §8's EVALUABLE test is numeric and measured; if it fails, the verdict is INCONCLUSIVE rather than a B1-only confirmation — which means a sparse control pool can void the question. That is the intended trade and it is stated before the fact.
9. **Sector plays no part in scoring, and the removal is measured to predate the window — this threat is closed, not merely listed.** The industry-rotation bonus is deleted and its columns are dead (§9), so a CLUSTER night is not an artifact of an explicit sector bonus. R1(a) dates commit `66cb63d` at **2026-05-14 10:24:45 ET**, roughly eight weeks before the window opens: **no part of the window predates it and the start-date shift this threat provided for does not fire.**
10. **Eight is a cap, not a count — and both tails exist.** `k_t = 8` is required for the primary (§2.3 step 2, the literal denominator of H-034's "≥ 4 of 8"; DP-25), which discards slates of other sizes. **Measured in-window: `k_t` = 4 → 1, 5 → 1, 8 → 32, 9 → 11, 10 → 1** — the `k_t > 8` tail is the larger one (12 of 68 nights over the report period), which the draft had not anticipated and which the primary discards symmetrically. A `k_t ≥ 6` primary, or a **relative** cut (`m_t / k_t ≥ 0.5`), is a **successor hypothesis** and is never a switch at the decision pass: with an *absolute* cut of four, a short slate is mechanically pushed toward DIVERSIFIED and a long one toward CLUSTER, so a mixed denominator would confound the arm with slate size.
11. **Matching on three features may not span selection** (Q006 threat 1). Post-match standardized mean differences are printed for B1 and B3 with every control-adjusted figure, and any `|SMD| > 0.25` on a matching feature is disclosed in the report.
12. **The dispersion secondary is the part a reader will most want to act on, and it decides nothing.** DP-44 forbids inventing an MPE for it. §9 forbids any rule resting on it alone. The report states both facts in the same paragraph as the numbers.
