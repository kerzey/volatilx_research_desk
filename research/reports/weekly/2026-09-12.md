## 1. Basis and method (read before the tables)

- **Population.**
  - Published SAS picks (`qualification_reason='selected'`): 907 picks over 112 nights, Apr 1 – Sep 10. The 8 `selected_dark` shadow picks are left out.
  - **Excluded** (`exclusions_v001.json`): the manual re-run nights 05-11, 05-12, 05-13, 05-14 and 07-06, which are 41 picks. The non-trading day 04-03 had no picks.
  - That leaves 107 nights. Trading nights come from SPY bars in the price freeze.
- **Return (changed from last week).**
  - The return runs from the next session's open to the close of session t+60 (W60) or t+20 (W20).
  - Split-adjusted bars from `p001_daily_split`. The sign is flipped for bearish picks, so every figure is a direction-adjusted %.
  - This replaces last week's close-based platform column. Across all 383 paired 60-day picks the two agree closely: correlation 0.996, mean gap −0.30 points.
  - It is a fixed-horizon return, so under Rule 5 it is **secondary and descriptive**.
- **Maturity.**
  - **W60:** pick dates ≤ 2026-06-15; 48 nights, 383 picks.
  - **W20 (NON_QUOTABLE):** pick dates ≤ 2026-08-12; 87 nights, 699 picks.
  - W60 is below the 80-night floor, so every W60 figure is descriptive.
- **Cells.**
  - Picks are averaged with equal weight within each night, and n counts nights.
  - Any cell with fewer than 20 nights prints SUPPRESSED.
- **CI.** Stationary block bootstrap over the nightly series (`eval_utils.stationary_block_bootstrap_ci`, seed 20260909, 2,000 reps), block length min(h, max(2, n/4)).
  - Overlapping 60-day windows make these **intervals too narrow**: 48 W60 nights hold less than one independent 60-day window.
- **L1/L2 touch (changed from last week).**
  - L1 = day-lane T1 and L2 = day-lane T2, taken from `sas_selection_excursion` v2 `upside_levels_json`. Each is converted to the split-adjusted basis.
  - **Window:** 20 sessions (the ladder's day-lane window, `sas_conviction_card.py:190`), counted from the next open.
  - **Scoring:** a level already beyond the open is **not a hit** (Rule 5) but stays in the denominator. A hit means a later intraday high (low, for bearish picks) reached the level.
  - **Excluded:** 38 of the 699 W20 picks (25 of 383 W60 picks), mostly for having no ladder row.
  - **Comparison:** on the same 661 picks, the platform's close-based rate (`level_hit_steps_json` ≤ 20) is L1 88% / L2 81%, against L1 70% / L2 72% here.
  - **L2 above L1 is an artifact of the rule.** L1 is "already passed at the open" on 21% of picks; L2 on 10%.
  - **No distance-matched control** is applied (Q006's job). These columns are descriptive only.
- **Regime (point-in-time).**
  - Source: the `market_regime_daily` v1.2 row written the same ET evening, which exists only from 2026-06-09. The 07-06 label was written late, and that night is excluded anyway.
  - Labels on record: 50 `strongly_bullish` and 14 `bullish` nights through 09-10. Inside W20 maturity that is 30 and 14.
  - Earlier nights are grouped as "no PIT label", which is not a regime stratum.
- **Source membership.**
  - Engines are UOA, projection and whale. A pick is single-engine or multi-engine by how many engines chose it.
  - A pick chosen on two or more UOA lanes counts as single-engine and is broken out separately.
