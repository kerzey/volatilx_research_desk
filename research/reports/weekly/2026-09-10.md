## 1. Basis and method (read before the tables)

- **Population.** Picks with `qualification_reason='selected'`: 907 picks over 112 nights, Apr 1 – Sep 10. The 8 `selected_dark` shadow picks are excluded.
- **Return.** The platform's `outcome_return_{60,20}d_w60`. In the code (`services/outcome_window.py:181`) this is a raw close-to-close return from the signal-date close. I flipped the sign for bearish picks, so every number is a direction-adjusted return in %.
- **Deviation from Rule 5.** The desk objective is next-open → T+h close. The freeze has no OHLC price table, so a next-open entry cannot be computed. Every number here includes the overnight gap after the pick night.
- **Maturity.** Maturity boundaries are clean:
  - **W60:** pick dates ≤ 2026-06-15, giving 52 nights and 415 picks. One pick, BK on 2026-04-16, has no W60 return because its price series ended at 24 bars.
  - **W20 (NON_QUOTABLE):** pick dates ≤ 2026-08-12, giving 92 nights and 740 picks.
- **Unit and cells.**
  - **Nights:** each cell is an equal-weight average of picks per night, and n counts nights. Cells with fewer than 20 nights print SUPPRESSED.
  - **W60 floor:** W60 in total (52 nights) is below the 80-night floor, so every W60 figure is descriptive only.
- **CI.** A stationary block bootstrap over the nightly series (`eval_utils.stationary_block_bootstrap_ci`, seed 20260909, 2,000 reps). Block length is min(horizon, max(2, n/4)) nights.
  - **Caveat:** each 60-day window overlaps almost every other night's window, so the 52 W60 nights hold less than one independent window. **The CIs understate the uncertainty.**
- **L1/L2 touch.** Whether the day-lane T1 or T2 price traded within the window (≤ 60 or ≤ 20 trading days). Taken from `level_hit_steps_json`, touched intraday.
  - **Descriptive only.** L1 is usually touched on day 1 (median step = 1), so a high L1 rate carries little information.
  - **Excluded from the denominators:** 58 picks for L1 and 60 for L2. 51 of these have no ladder row (the known null-ladder issue) and the rest have no day-lane target.
- **Regime (point-in-time).** The `market_regime_daily` v1.2 row for the pick date, created the same ET evening at about 16:05.
  - **Coverage:** such a label exists only from 2026-06-09.
  - **2026-07-06 excluded:** its label was written 07-07 at 10:23 ET, after decision time.
  - **Only two regimes appeared on those nights:** `strongly_bullish` and `bullish`.
  - **Earlier nights:** grouped as "no PIT label". That group is shown for completeness and is not a regime stratum.
- **Source membership.** Single-engine vs multi-engine, where the engines are UOA, projection and whale. A pick on two UOA lanes (e.g. day + swing) counts as single-engine and is broken out separately.
- **No market benchmark.** SPY is not in the frozen dataset, so there is no market-relative column.

## 8. Patterns proposed for the backlog

These are hypotheses, not findings. They are **not yet written** to `research/BACKLOG.md` because this run was not allowed to write files; someone needs to add them. The data mixes in-sample and out-of-sample periods, so each needs a PREREG before anyone tests it.

- **H-004 (F1 Selection edge): bearish SAS picks lose money after direction adjustment.**
  - **Seen:** W20 −8.47% [−15.46, −2.14] over 24 nights / 38 picks; L2 touch 49% vs 80% for bullish picks.
  - **Baseline:** bullish picks on the same nights, and zero.
  - **Mechanism:** bearish setups fighting a bull tape.
  - **Noise risk:** small n; 25 of 45 bearish picks were in April, so this is essentially one period.
- **H-013 (F2 Calibration): the 80–90 band underperforms on nights labelled `strongly_bullish` (point-in-time).**
  - **Seen:** W20 −3.04% [−5.80, −0.37] over 30 nights.
  - **Baseline:** the same band on bullish or neutral point-in-time nights, plus the projection-bullish list on the same nights (−1.18%). The second baseline checks whether the effect is tape-wide.
  - **Mechanism:** a late-trend exhaustion effect.
  - **Noise risk:** regime and calendar are fully confounded, and only 14 bullish nights exist. This probably needs prospective data before a PREREG is viable.
- **Extend H-011 (F2) to projection confidence labels.**
  - **Seen:** low-confidence projection-bullish picks beat high-confidence ones: W60 +60.9% vs +18.3%, and W20 +16.9% vs +3.7%.
  - **Noise risk:** outlier-prone, with 72 low-confidence picks and very wide CIs.
- **Relevant to existing items, no new entry needed:**
  - **H-010 (score-band monotonicity):** the 70–80 vs 80–90 gap is a calendar effect, since the 70–80 band has essentially stopped appearing after May.
  - **H-051 (lane choice):** the swing lane was −0.71% on W60, against +12.78% for short and +7.16% for long.
